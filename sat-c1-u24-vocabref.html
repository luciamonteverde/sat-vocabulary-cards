Vocabulary Reference

Harm, Illness & Destruction — Part 1

Conflagration

/kon-fluh-GRAY-shun/ noun

Conflagration means a large, destructive fire.

Laceration

/lass-uh-RAY-shun/ noun

Laceration means a deep cut or tear in flesh.

Analgesic

/an-ul-JEE-zik/ noun

Analgesic means a substance that relieves pain.

Deleterious

/del-ih-TEER-ee-us/ adjective

Deleterious means harmful, often in a subtle or gradual way.

Antiseptic

/an-tih-SEP-tik/ adjective

Antiseptic means clean and free of harmful germs; sterile.

Pernicious

/per-NISH-us/ adjective

Pernicious means causing gradual, insidious, and serious harm.

Noxious

/NOK-shus/ adjective

Noxious means harmful or poisonous, especially to breathe or be exposed to.

Corrosive

/kuh-ROH-siv/ adjective

Corrosive means tending to erode, eat away, or destroy something gradually through chemical action.

Grievous

/GREE-vus/ adjective

Grievous means very serious and severe, especially describing harm, injury, or damage.

Detrimental

/det-rih-MEN-tul/ adjective

Detrimental means causing harm or damage.

Combustion

/kum-BUS-chun/ noun

Combustion means the process of burning.

Pathology

/puh-THOL-uh-jee/ noun

Pathology means a deviation from a normal, healthy condition; the study of disease.

Hazardous

/HAZ-er-dus/ adjective

Hazardous means involving risk or danger, especially of harm.

Devastated

/DEV-uh-stay-tid/ adjective

Devastated means severely damaged, destroyed, or overwhelmed — by disaster, shock, or grief.

Destructive

/dih-STRUK-tiv/ adjective

Destructive means causing great and often irreparable damage.
