Vocabulary Reference

Nature, Habitat & Living Things — Part 1

Sinuous

/SIN-yoo-us/ adjective

Sinuous means winding and curving in a graceful, snake-like way.

Acidification

/uh-sid-ih-fih-KAY-shun/ noun

Acidification means the process of becoming more chemically acidic, a term used especially of water chemistry.

Anthropogenic

/an-thruh-puh-JEN-ik/ adjective

Anthropogenic means caused or produced by human activity.

Viscous

/VIS-kus/ adjective

Viscous means thick, sticky, and slow-flowing rather than free-flowing.

Hibernation

/hy-bur-NAY-shun/ noun

Hibernation means a dormant, inactive state some animals enter to survive winter, marked by lowered body temperature and slowed metabolism.

Instinctive

/in-STINK-tiv/ adjective

Instinctive means arising automatically from instinct rather than from learning, reasoning, or experience.

Taxonomy

/tak-SON-uh-mee/ noun

Taxonomy means the scientific practice of classifying organisms into ordered categories.

Nomadic

/noh-MAD-ik/ adjective

Nomadic means wandering from place to place rather than settling permanently.

Parasitic

/par-uh-SIT-ik/ adjective

Parasitic means living on or in a host organism and benefiting at that host's expense.

Topography

/tuh-POG-ruh-fee/ noun

Topography means the arrangement of an area's physical features, especially its elevation and shape.

Feral

/FEER-ul/ adjective

Feral means wild and untamed, especially of a domesticated species now living without human contact.

Congenital

/kun-JEN-ih-tul/ adjective

Congenital means present from birth, usually describing a condition or trait.

Rustic

/RUS-tik/ adjective

Rustic means characteristic of the plain, simple countryside, unsophisticated in a homespun way.

Endemic

/en-DEM-ik/ adjective

Endemic means native to, and found characteristically only in, one particular place or region.

Mountainous

/MOWN-tuh-nus/ adjective

Mountainous means having many mountains, or relating to high, rugged terrain.

Predatory

/PRED-uh-tor-ee/ adjective

Predatory means living by hunting and killing other animals for food.

Biodiversity

/BY-oh-dih-VUR-sih-tee/ noun

Biodiversity means the variety of plant and animal life found in a habitat or across the world.

Terrestrial

/tuh-RES-tree-ul/ adjective

Terrestrial means relating to or living on land, as opposed to in water or in the air.

Hereditary

/huh-RED-ih-ter-ee/ adjective

Hereditary means passed down genetically from parent to offspring.

Aquatic

/uh-KWAT-ik/ adjective

Aquatic means living or growing in water, or relating to water.

Pastoral

/PAS-tuh-rul/ adjective

Pastoral means relating to the peaceful, idealized life of the countryside, especially farming or shepherding.

Vegetation

/vej-ih-TAY-shun/ noun

Vegetation means plant life considered collectively, especially that of a particular region.

Ecological

/ee-kuh-LOJ-ih-kul/ adjective

Ecological means relating to the relationships between living organisms and their environment.

Fertility

/fur-TIL-ih-tee/ noun

Fertility means the capacity to produce abundant offspring, crops, or vegetation.

Atmospheric

/at-mus-FEER-ik/ adjective

Atmospheric means relating to the atmosphere, the layer of gases surrounding a planet.

Inherited

/in-HER-ih-tid/ adjective

Inherited means received genetically or through family lineage.

Migration

/my-GRAY-shun/ noun

Migration means the seasonal movement of animals, or of people, from one region to another.

Indigenous

/in-DIJ-uh-nus/ adjective

Indigenous means originating naturally in a particular place; native to that place.

Coastal

/KOH-stul/ adjective

Coastal means relating to, or located near, a coast.

Conservation

/kon-sur-VAY-shun/ noun

Conservation means the protection and careful management of the environment and natural resources.

Agricultural

/ag-rih-KUL-chur-ul/ adjective

Agricultural means relating to farming and the cultivation of land.

Rural

/ROOR-ul/ adjective

Rural means relating to the countryside, as distinct from towns and cities.
