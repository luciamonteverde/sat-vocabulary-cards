Repetition

/rep-uh-TISH-un/ — rhymes with nutrition · noun

Hear It
What It Means

Repetition is the act of doing or saying again something that has already occurred.

Show it in context

She insisted that only through this steady repetition would the movements sink into my hands so deeply that I would no longer have to think about them.

Here's how to decide when repetition is the exact word.

Worked Example

The old song played in the grocery store—the same verse, the same chorus, repeating four times before the song ended—making her hum it for hours afterward.

Repetition is more precise than 'repeating' or 'repetitiveness' because it is the noun form that names the act itself as a thing, a technique, something that can be used intentionally. In this song context, the repetition is what makes the tune stick.

Synonyms · words that mean the same — tap one to see how it differs
iteration

Iteration is a single instance of a repeated cycle; repetition is the ongoing act of doing the same thing again and again.

recurrence

Recurrence is something happening again occasionally; repetition emphasizes the purposeful, continuous doing of something multiple times.

replication

Replication is creating an exact copy of something; repetition is the act of doing something over and over.

Antonyms · opposites

originality — Creating something new and different, not doing the same thing again.

Build the Word

rep- (again) + pet- (to seek, do) Latin: 'to do again, to seek again'

Repetition comes from the Latin repetere—to do again. The root rep- means 'again' and petere means 'to seek or do.' Repetition is literally 'doing again'—performing the same action multiple times until it becomes familiar.

The root connection →

Petition and repetition both trace back to the Latin root petere ('to seek, ask, to do'). A petition is formally seeking or asking (making a request); repetition is seeking or doing again (doing something multiple times) — same root of action, different applications.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

neutral

Repetition is a straightforward, everyday word that can be used in positive contexts (learning through repetition) or negative ones (the repetition gets boring). When you need to emphasize doing something again and again—whether for learning, practice, or effect—repetition is your word. For a single instance of something happening again, 'recurrence' is better; for the idea of repeating a pattern, 'pattern' might be simpler.
