Incremental

/in-KREM-en-tul/ — rhymes with sentimental · adjective

Hear It
What It Means

Incremental means increasing or developing little by little, in small, regular steps.

Show it in context

The company's price increases were incremental, so small each month that customers barely noticed them add up.

Here's how to decide when incremental is the exact word.

Worked Example

The engineer improved the software by fixing one small bug per week, and after fifty weeks, the performance had nearly doubled.

Incremental means not just small changes, but SMALL REGULAR STEPS THAT ADD UP over time. The engineer's weekly bug fixes are a perfect example: each fix is tiny, but the regularity and accumulation over fifty weeks created a major improvement. That precision — 'small, repeated steps that compound' — is exactly what incremental names.

Synonyms · words that mean the same — tap one to see how it differs
gradual

Gradual means slow and steady change over time. Incremental is more precise: it emphasizes SMALL, REGULAR STEPS that add up, not just any slow change.

progressive

Progressive means advancing or developing forward. Incremental is more specific: it's about measurable, small steps upward (price, performance), not just forward motion.

continuous

Continuous means unbroken and ongoing. Incremental means changes happen in discrete small steps; there may be pauses or gaps between them.

Antonyms · opposites

sudden — Sudden means happening all at once without warning. Incremental is the opposite: changes arrive in small, regular steps over time.

Build the Word

cresc- Latin: 'grow, increase'

The cresc- root means 'to grow or increase' — incremental changes are small growths that compound over time, each one a tiny increase that builds into something big.

The cheers grew louder and louder until they reached a crescendo at the moment the team scored.

The root connection →

Incremental and crescendo share the root cresc- ('grow, increase'). In incremental, that root gives us small, regular steps of growth; in crescendo, it gives us a gradual increase building to a peak.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

neutral / formal

Incremental is a business and science word for steady, small-step improvements. In everyday speech, say 'gradual' or 'little by little.' Use incremental when emphasizing that progress comes through repeated small additions, not sudden jumps.
