Limited

/LIM-ih-tid/ adjective

Hear It
What It Means

Limited means restricted in size, amount, or extent; not extensive

Show it in context

Limited means restricted in size, amount, or extent — like a tiny diner whose menu offers only five dishes, and not one more.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

Jae's phone plan comes with limited data, so he saves videos for home Wi-Fi to stay under the monthly cap.

The plan sets a fixed boundary on how much data Jae can use. Limited signals that a definite cap or border restricts the amount — cross it and you're cut off.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

Neutral / everyday

Limited stresses that a boundary has been set on purpose: a limited menu, limited seating, a limited-time offer. It pairs naturally with "to" for whoever falls inside the line — entry is limited to members. Compared with plain small, limited implies someone or something drew the line.
