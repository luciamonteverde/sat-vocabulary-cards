Vocabulary Reference

Determination, Effort & Ambition — Part 1

Obdurate

/OB-duh-rit/ adjective

Obdurate means unyielding to persuasion, pity, or moral appeals.

Consecrate

/KON-sih-krayt/ verb

Consecrate means to dedicate something formally to a holy or serious purpose.

Balk

/BAWK/ verb

Balk means to stop abruptly and refuse to continue, especially at an obstacle.

Obstinate

/OB-stih-nit/ adjective

Obstinate means not yielding easily to argument or persuasion; stubborn.

Painstaking

/PAYNZ-tay-king/ adjective

Painstaking means taking great pains or care; extremely thorough and careful.

Consummate

/KON-suh-mayt/ verb

Consummate means to complete or bring to fulfillment, as a deal, agreement, or marriage.

Abstain

/ab-STAYN/ verb

Abstain means to freely choose not to do something, especially through self-restraint.

Fortitude

/FOR-tih-tood/ noun

Fortitude means strength of mind in facing danger, pain, or adversity; courage under strain.

Diligent

/DIL-ih-jent/ adjective

Diligent means showing steady, careful effort in one's work.

Adamant

/AD-uh-ment/ adjective

Adamant means refusing to be persuaded or to change one's mind; unyielding.

Hesitant

/HEZ-ih-tent/ adjective

Hesitant means slow to act because of uncertainty; reluctant.

Cultivate

/KUL-tih-vayt/ verb

Cultivate means to nurture, develop, or improve through sustained effort.

Aspire

/uh-SPYRE/ verb

Aspire means to direct one's hopes or ambitions toward achieving something.

Pilgrimage

/PIL-grim-ij/ noun

Pilgrimage means a long, purposeful journey undertaken for a deeply meaningful goal, often spiritual.

Persist

/per-SIST/ verb

Persist means to continue firmly, especially in spite of difficulty or opposition.

Vanguard

/VAN-gard/ noun

Vanguard means the foremost or leading position in a movement, field, or advancing group.

Attain

/uh-TAYN/ verb

Attain means to succeed in achieving something through effort.

Ambition

/am-BISH-un/ noun

Ambition means a strong desire to achieve something, requiring determination.

Earnest

/UR-nist/ adjective

Earnest means showing sincere and serious intent; not joking or half-hearted.

Rash

/RASH/ adjective

Rash means acting with excessive haste and without proper consideration; reckless.

Hesitate

/HEZ-ih-tayt/ verb

Hesitate means to pause or show reluctance before acting, from uncertainty.

Persistent

/per-SIS-tent/ adjective

Persistent means continuing firmly despite difficulty or opposition.

Sustained

/suh-STAYND/ adjective

Sustained means continuing for an extended period without weakening or stopping.

Acquired

/uh-KWYRD/ adjective

Acquired means gained through one's own effort or experience rather than present from birth.
