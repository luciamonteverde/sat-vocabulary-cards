Vocabulary Reference

Suffix -ive/-ative/-itive (adjective-forming)

Connive

/kuh-NYVE/ verb

Connive means to secretly cooperate or plot with others in a scheme, often a dishonest one.

Corroborative

/kuh-ROB-er-uh-tiv/ adjective

Corroborative means serving to support, confirm, or strengthen a claim or piece of evidence.

Impassive

/im-PASS-iv/ adjective

Impassive means showing no emotion; calm and unmoved, especially under stress or provocation.

Restive

/RES-tiv/ adjective

Restive means restless, impatient, or resistant to control or delay, especially while being made to wait.

Invective

/in-VEK-tiv/ noun

Invective means a sharp, angry verbal attack; harsh, insulting language.

Effusive

/ih-FYOO-siv/ adjective

Effusive means expressing feelings in an unrestrained, gushing, overflowing way.

Plaintive

/PLAYN-tiv/ adjective

Plaintive means sounding sad or mournful, especially describing a sound, voice, or cry.

Furtive

/FUR-tiv/ adjective

Furtive means done secretly or stealthily, in a way that suggests hidden or guilty intent.

Discursive

/dis-KUR-siv/ adjective

Discursive means rambling from topic to topic; lacking a clear, direct structure.

Pensive

/PEN-siv/ adjective

Pensive means engaged in deep, often somewhat wistful or melancholy thought.

Pejorative

/pih-JOR-uh-tiv/ adjective

Pejorative means expressing contempt or disapproval; describing a term that carries a negative, insulting sense.

Incisive

/in-SY-siv/ adjective

Incisive means direct, sharp, and precise, especially in analysis or comment; cutting straight to the point.

Reclusive

/rih-KLOO-siv/ adjective

Reclusive means living in deliberate solitude, avoiding the company of others.

Generative

/JEN-er-uh-tiv/ adjective

Generative means having the power to originate, produce, or create something new.

Prescriptive

/prih-SKRIP-tiv/ adjective

Prescriptive means laying down rules for how something should be done, rather than describing how it actually is.

Exploitative

/ek-SPLOY-tuh-tiv/ adjective

Exploitative means taking unfair advantage of someone or something for one's own benefit.

Combative

/kum-BAT-iv/ adjective

Combative means eager or ready to fight, argue, or resist.

Contemplative

/kun-TEM-pluh-tiv/ adjective

Contemplative means involved in or characterized by deep, calm, unhurried thought or reflection.

Instructive

/in-STRUK-tiv/ adjective

Instructive means serving to teach, inform, or provide useful insight.

Vindictive

/vin-DIK-tiv/ adjective

Vindictive means having or showing a strong desire for revenge; vengeful.

Apprehensive

/ap-rih-HEN-siv/ adjective

Apprehensive means anxious or fearful about something that might happen.

Submissive

/sub-MISS-iv/ adjective

Submissive means readily yielding to the will or authority of another.

Qualitative

/KWAH-lih-tay-tiv/ adjective

Qualitative means relating to quality or kind, rather than to quantity.

Lucrative

/LOO-kruh-tiv/ adjective

Lucrative means producing a large amount of profit; very profitable.

Responsive

/rih-SPON-siv/ adjective

Responsive means reacting quickly and positively to something.

Quantitative

/KWAHN-tih-tay-tiv/ adjective

Quantitative means relating to quantity or amount, rather than to quality.

Comparative

/kum-PAIR-uh-tiv/ adjective

Comparative means relating to or based on comparison between things.

Defensive

/dih-FEN-siv/ adjective

Defensive means used or intended to protect against attack; overly protective of oneself when criticized.
