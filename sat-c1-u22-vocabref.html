Vocabulary Reference

Joy, Enthusiasm & Praise — Part 1

Idolatrous

/eye-DOL-uh-truss/ adjective

Idolatrous means worshipping someone or something excessively, as if it were a god — a word with a built-in warning.

Adulation

/aj-uh-LAY-shun/ noun

Adulation means excessive, gushing, often fawning praise or admiration.

Vivacious

/vih-VAY-shuss/ adjective

Vivacious means full of lively, charming energy and spirit, especially in social settings.

Euphoric

/yoo-FOR-ik/ adjective

Euphoric means in a sustained state of intense elation and well-being, often almost dreamlike.

Boisterous

/BOY-stur-uss/ adjective

Boisterous means loud, energetic, and exuberant, often to the point of rowdiness.

Meritorious

/mair-ih-TOR-ee-uss/ adjective

Meritorious means deserving of praise or reward because of genuine merit or achievement.

Commendation

/kom-en-DAY-shun/ noun

Commendation means a formal, official statement or act of praise, such as an award or citation.

Celebratory

/SEL-uh-bruh-tor-ee/ adjective

Celebratory means relating to or marking a celebration; festive in purpose.

Auspicious

/aw-SPISH-uss/ adjective

Auspicious means favorable; suggesting that success is likely to come — a promising sign, not present-tense joy.

Revere

/rih-VEER/ verb

Revere means to feel or show deep, respectful admiration for someone or something.

Renown

/rih-NOWN/ noun

Renown means widespread fame combined with a good reputation.

Revel

/REV-ul/ verb

Revel means to enjoy something with unrestrained, exuberant enthusiasm — often used as 'revel in.'

Venerable

/VEN-ur-uh-bul/ adjective

Venerable means deserving deep respect because of age, wisdom, or great achievement.

Relish

/REL-ish/ verb

Relish means to enjoy something greatly, savoring it with real appreciation.

Ecstatic

/ek-STAT-ik/ adjective

Ecstatic means feeling or showing an intense, overpowering burst of joy, usually in reaction to specific news or a specific moment.

Reputable

/REP-yuh-tuh-bul/ adjective

Reputable means having a good, trustworthy reputation; respectable and legitimate.

Festive

/FES-tiv/ adjective

Festive means suited to a celebration; joyful and lively in atmosphere.

Optimistic

/op-tih-MIS-tik/ adjective

Optimistic means hopeful and confident about future outcomes.

Celebrity

/suh-LEB-rih-tee/ noun

Celebrity means a widely known, famous person, especially in entertainment or public life.

Celebration

/sel-uh-BRAY-shun/ noun

Celebration means an event or occasion marked by festivities in honor of something.

Impressive

/im-PRESS-iv/ adjective

Impressive means grand, skillful, or capable of making a strong, admiring impact.
