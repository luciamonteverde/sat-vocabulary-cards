Ancillary

AN-suh-ler-ee adjective

Hear It
What It Means

Ancillary means serving a supporting or secondary role rather than a central one.

Show it in context

The main lecture ran ninety minutes, but the professor treated the slides as ancillary, useful only if a student happened to miss a word.

See How It Works · a worked example, then try one yourself
Worked Example

The firm's paralegals managed the scheduling, filed every document, and gathered background research so that the lead attorneys could pour all their energy into arguing the case in court.

Ancillary describes something that plays a SUPPORTING OR SECONDARY ROLE beside a clear main thing — here, the paralegals' work exists to help the lead attorneys' central task of arguing the case. But ancillary does NOT mean 'unimportant' or 'irrelevant.' Ancillary things STILL GENUINELY MATTER and actively HELP the main work succeed; they are simply not the CENTRAL focus. The paralegals provide essential support, yet the case argued in court — not the filing and scheduling — is the heart of the operation.

Your Turn

A food truck has exactly one cook, who personally prepares every single dish the truck sells. Without that cook, the truck has nothing at all to serve to customers. Would 'ancillary' correctly describe the cook's role in the food truck's business?

Build the Word · tap each piece to reveal its meaning
Rootancill-maidservant, helper (Latin ancilla — a whole borrowed noun, not built from smaller pieces a modern reader can reuse)
Suffix-aryrelating to, connected with

In a Roman household, an ancilla was a maidservant — a helper whose entire role was to support the family's main business. Add the adjective suffix -ary ('relating to, connected with') and ancillary literally means 'like a maidservant's work': genuinely useful, actively helping, but always in service of something more central. The root ancilla was borrowed into English whole; it isn't built from smaller pieces you will meet in other everyday English words, so the maidservant image itself is the thing to remember.

Use the root in a new word: ancilla

In quantum computing, an ancilla is an extra helper qubit that assists the main calculation — the very same Latin 'helper' hiding inside ancillary.

Word Choice · its tone, and when to use it

formal

Ancillary is a formal word most at home in academic, business, legal, medical, and technical writing (for example, 'ancillary services' or 'ancillary staff'). In everyday conversation people usually reach for plainer words like 'extra,' 'side,' or 'supporting' instead.
