Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Hiatus means a break or gap in continuity.

See the word in context

The bakery's window sign apologizes for a two-month hiatus while the ovens are rebuilt, and promises the sourdough will be back.

The two chess players' correspondence game ran from 1969 to 2004 with one hiatus: nine years in the middle, after a move mailed to Buenos Aires never arrived and each man quietly assumed the other had given up. A granddaughter found the unanswered postcard in a drawer in 1987, posted it with a note, and the game picked up at move 31 as though nothing had happened.

Hiatus is the right word because it means a break or gap in continuity — the game was never over, merely interrupted, and both the before and the after belong to one continuous whole. A pause lasts moments; a hiatus can swallow years; but both differ from an ending, because a hiatus implies the thread will be, or at least could be, taken up again.

Section 2

Accuracy

“Does this word fit the meaning exactly, or does a neighbour fit better?”

Compare the words

Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

gap: A gap is the all-purpose opening in continuity — in a fence, a career, a data set. A hiatus is a particular kind of gap: a break in something serial, usually a long one, where the run is understood to continue afterward. Every hiatus is a gap; most gaps are not hiatuses.

interlude: An interlude is a stretch between two parts of something, and it brings content and a character of its own — the music between acts, the comic scene between battles. A hiatus is empty by contrast: the run simply stops, and the gap holds nothing until the thread resumes.

What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

continuity: the unbroken flow itself

unbroken run: a stretch with no gap anywhere in it

Where it sits · how lasting the interruption is

pause → hiatus → cessation

pause vs. hiatus: A pause is the mildest rung — over in moments, barely denting the flow; a hiatus opens a real gap, measured in months or years.

cessation vs. hiatus: A cessation goes further than a hiatus: a hiatus ends with the thread picked up again, while a cessation closes the activity down for good.
