archaic

adjective · ar-KAY-ik

Section 1

Meaning

Archaic means belonging to an earlier period of time and no longer in current use.

In her grandmother’s attic, Maya found archaic letters written in a spidery hand, words she’d never seen before because the language itself had changed so much.

Archaic means something is not just old, but specifically old and no longer in use. It’s more precise than “ancient” (which emphasizes age but not disuse) or “old” (which says nothing about whether the thing is still used).
