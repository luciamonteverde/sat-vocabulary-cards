acquiesce

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Acquiesce means to go along with something without protesting.

See the word in context

Though she thought the mural's colors were wrong for the lobby, the painter acquiesced and mixed them as ordered.

The sous-chef believed the new tasting menu was too long. He said so once, quietly; when the owner kept all nine courses, he acquiesced, tying his apron and working the line without another word.

Acquiesce is not plain agreement — it means going along without protest while your own doubts stay put. The reluctance is the point: you accept the decision outwardly and keep the objection to yourself.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    comply: Comply means doing what a rule or request says — neutral, paperwork-level obedience with no feeling attached either way. Acquiesce adds the swallowed objection: to acquiesce is to go along without protest while privately still disagreeing. You can comply cheerfully; nobody acquiesces cheerfully.

    capitulate: Capitulate means to stop resisting and give in, usually after open opposition and under real pressure — a besieged city, a holdout who finally folds. Acquiesce is quieter: there was never an open fight, only a doubt kept private while you go along.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    object: to speak your disagreement out loud

    defy: to refuse openly and push back

    Where it sits · how much of your will you surrender

    humor → acquiesce → submit

    humor vs. acquiesce: To humor someone is much milder than to acquiesce — humoring plays along for a moment, mostly to keep the peace, while acquiesce means genuinely accepting a decision your own judgment still doubts.

    submit vs. acquiesce: Submit goes further than acquiesce — acquiescing swallows one decision; submitting hands over your will across the board.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      formal

      Acquiesce is the word for giving way without a fight — the objection is swallowed, not solved. Reach for it when the reluctance matters. Its unit-mates sit on either side: accede means granting a request, often after open resistance, and concede means admitting a point. Only acquiesce insists on the silence.
