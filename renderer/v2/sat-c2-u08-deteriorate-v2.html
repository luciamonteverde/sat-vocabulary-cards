deteriorate

/dih-TEER-ee-uh-rayt/ - the stress falls on TEER

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Deteriorate means to become progressively worse in condition or quality.

See the word in context

Her night vision deteriorated so gradually that she noticed only when the streetlights began wearing halos.

The county's gravel roads deteriorate on a schedule the crews know by heart: washboard ripples by June, potholes by August, and by the first freeze a lane the school bus takes at walking speed. No single storm wrecks them; each week is only a little worse than the last, which is exactly the problem.

Deteriorate is the exact word because it names the slide, not the crash: becoming progressively worse in condition or quality. A washout is one bad night; deterioration is a hundred forgettable ones. If you can name the single moment it broke, the word overshoots — deteriorate is for damage with no headline.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    worsen: Worsen is the flattest report: things got worse, by any amount, at any speed — one storm can worsen a road overnight. Deteriorate insists on a slide — condition failing progressively, step after step, over time. Every deteriorating road is worsening, but a single bad night is not a slide.

    degrade: Degrade is the technician's word for being broken down step by step — sunlight degrades plastics, wear degrades a signal, misuse degrades a battery — and it points at a cause doing the breaking. Deteriorate simply tracks the downhill slide itself, whatever mix of causes lies behind it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    improve: to get better in condition or quality

    recover: to climb back to a former, healthier state

    Where it sits · how far down the slide has gone

    slip → deteriorate → crumble

    slip vs. deteriorate: Standards can slip for a bad week and be shored right back up; deteriorate is graver — a steady, continuing slide.

    crumble vs. deteriorate: Crumble is the far end of the slide deteriorate names: what was worsening by degrees finally comes apart in your hands.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / clinical

    Deteriorate is the general-purpose word for getting progressively worse — health, roads, negotiations, tape. When the cause is specifically disuse, this unit's atrophy is sharper; when nothing is getting worse but nothing is moving either, the word is stagnation. Deteriorate claims motion, and the motion is downhill.

    The lighthouse logbook, kept daily from 1871 to 1963, is the archive's most requested item — and its most fragile. Conservators are clear that the book never met with any single accident. No flood touched it, no fire, no careless reader. Instead its condition has simply deteriorated, year over year: the iron-gall ink browning a shade at a time, the paper edges crumbling a little further each decade. The archive now photographs every page annually, comparing the images to measure how far the fading has traveled since the last check. Visitors handle a facsimile. The original rests in a climate-controlled case, where the slow decline can at least be made slower still.
