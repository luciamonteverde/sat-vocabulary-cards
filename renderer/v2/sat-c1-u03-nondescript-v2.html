nondescript

/NON-dih-skript/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Nondescript means lacking any distinctive or interesting features; so plain and ordinary that nothing about it stands out.

See the word in context

The nondescript beige office building on the corner housed three separate law firms, yet most people who worked downtown couldn't have picked it out of a lineup.

After the merger, the company moved into a nondescript strip-mall unit between a dry cleaner and a phone-repair shop, choosing the anonymous location specifically so rival firms wouldn't notice how fast it was growing.

Nothing about the building's plain, generic exterior gave away the successful company operating inside — that forgettable blandness is exactly what "nondescript" describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    generic: Generic stresses that something could be any one of many identical things — a generic hotel room looks like every other hotel room. Nondescript makes a different point: nothing about the thing itself can be described or remembered, whether or not copies of it exist.

    anonymous: Anonymous means carrying no name or identity — an anonymous donor, an anonymous office tower with no sign on it. Nondescript is about features, not names: a nondescript building offers nothing worth noticing. A striking landmark can still be anonymous if no one knows what it is.

    forgettable: Forgettable says a thing slips out of memory the moment you look away. Nondescript explains why: there was never a distinctive feature to hold onto. One word describes the effect on you; the other describes the thing itself.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    distinctive: has a quality that sets it clearly apart

    striking: immediately catches the eye

    Where it sits · how little stands out about it

    plain → nondescript → unremarkable

    plain vs. nondescript: Something plain simply has no special decoration or feature, though a plain thing can still be easy to describe; nondescript goes further — nothing about it registers at all.

    unremarkable vs. nondescript: Something unremarkable goes a step further than nondescript, giving observers nothing at all worth commenting on.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral, descriptive

    Nondescript is a neutral, everyday word — not quite an insult, but it does suggest blandness. It's most often used for buildings, vehicles, clothing, or appearances that blend into the background.
