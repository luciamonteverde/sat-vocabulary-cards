antiquated

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Antiquated means old and out of date.

See the word in context

The hotel's antiquated switchboard still routes calls through brass plugs, and only one retired operator understands its wiring.

Downtown's traffic lights still run on mechanical timers set decades ago: every intersection turns green on its own fixed schedule, whether forty cars are waiting or none. Engineers call the system antiquated rather than broken — it does exactly what it was built to do. The trouble is that what it was built to do no longer matches the streets above it.

Antiquated is the right word because it means old and out of date — old in a way that no longer serves. Age alone does not qualify: a cast-iron pan from the same era still works beautifully and is merely old. Antiquated adds the verdict that the world has moved past the thing.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    outmoded: Outmoded means pushed aside by newer ways of doing the job, and it points at the method or the fashion — an outmoded technique, an outmoded cut of suit. Antiquated aims at the whole thing — machine, system, law — and adds that its age has begun costing you.

    old-school: Old-school describes older ways kept on with a wink of respect — an old-school barber, an old-school defense. The age is a feature, savored on purpose. Antiquated turns the same age into a complaint: the thing has fallen behind, and keeping it going has started to cost.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    up-to-date: current, and still fit for today's use

    state-of-the-art: the newest and most capable available

    Where it sits · how far out of use it is

    dated → antiquated → extinct

    dated vs. antiquated: Dated is the milder judgment — a dated kitchen shows its age but cooks dinner fine; antiquated says the age has started costing you.

    extinct vs. antiquated: Extinct goes further than antiquated: antiquated equipment still runs, badly out of step with the present, while an extinct technology has disappeared from use altogether.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / critical

    Antiquated is the working complaint: old and out of date, past its usefulness. It judges function, where dated only notes style and outmoded blames fashion. Against its unit-mates: antediluvian makes the same point as a joke about sheer age, and an anachronism is not necessarily old at all — it is in the wrong period. An antiquated filing system sits squarely, and uselessly, in its own present.
