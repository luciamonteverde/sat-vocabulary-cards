Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Sequential means following in a fixed order, one after another.

See the word in context

Karate ranks are sequential; no student tests for brown until green is earned.

A rocket to orbit is a sequential machine. The first stage must burn out, shut down, and fall away; only then does the next dare light — fire both together and the ship tears itself apart. Mission control's checklist runs the same way — every step arms the one after it, and there is no step nine until step eight is complete.

Sequential is the exact word because it names what follows in a fixed order, one after another — not merely arranged, but chained, each stage depending on the last. Ordered says things have places; sequential says the places must be visited in line. Where order is survival, sequential is the word engineers reach for.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    consecutive: Consecutive counts pure adjacency: things back-to-back with nothing between, like three consecutive wins or five consecutive seats. It says nothing about a required order. Sequential adds the requirement — the stages must come in that order, each unlocked by the last.

    step-by-step: Step-by-step describes a process taken one stage at a time, and it is the friendly, instruction-manual way to say so — a recipe, a tutorial, a patient teacher walking someone through. It makes no claim that the order is required. Sequential makes exactly that claim: skip a stage, or swap two, and the process breaks.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    jumbled: out of any order at all

    shuffled: mixed out of line on purpose

    Where it sits · how strictly each must follow the last

    ordered → sequential → lockstep

    ordered vs. sequential: An ordered shelf has a place for everything; sequential adds that the places must be taken one after another, in line.

    lockstep vs. sequential: Lockstep goes past sequential — not only must the steps come in order, nothing may drift even a beat out of pace.

    Word Choice

    formal / technical

    Sequential is the engineer's word for steps that must come one after another — assembly lines, safety drills, code. This unit's cyclical bends the line into a loop that returns; sequential runs its line once and ends. If the order is merely tidy rather than required, ordered or step-by-step is honest and lighter.
