piecemeal

/PEES-meel/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Piecemeal means done gradually in small, disconnected pieces — one fragment at a time, with no overall plan coordinating the parts.

See the word in context

The museum assembled the dinosaur skeleton piecemeal, one donated crate of bones at a time, across thirty scattered years.

Ana and Luis furnished their first apartment piecemeal. A kitchen table came from a March yard sale, two mismatched chairs from a cousin in June, a lamp rescued from a curb in October, a bookshelf the following spring. Three years in, no two pieces matched and two rooms still stood half empty — there had never been a plan, only whatever turned up, whenever it happened to turn up.

The furnishing arrived one stray piece at a time, with no design tying the pieces together — change in unplanned fragments over years, which is exactly what piecemeal describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    incremental: Incremental means advancing by small planned steps, each building on the last — incremental change follows a design. Piecemeal shares the small steps but drops the design: the pieces arrive as chance allows, with no plan tying them together.

    fragmentary: Fragmentary describes the state of a thing — existing in disconnected pieces, like a fragmentary manuscript. Piecemeal describes the process: work done one scrap at a time, so a piecemeal effort is what leaves a fragmentary result behind.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    wholesale: done all at once and on the full scale

    systematic: following a coordinated overall plan

    Where it sits · how much order survives in the process

    gradual → piecemeal → haphazard

    gradual vs. piecemeal: Gradual is weaker than piecemeal: gradual only says the change comes slowly, while piecemeal adds that it comes in disconnected scraps, with no plan holding the pieces together.

    haphazard vs. piecemeal: Haphazard goes further than piecemeal: piecemeal work still answers real needs one piece at a time, but haphazard work has lost order altogether — nothing decides what happens next.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    mildly critical in policy and planning contexts; neutral when simply describing method

    Piecemeal works as both adjective ('a piecemeal approach') and adverb ('reforms adopted piecemeal'). In SAT passages it usually carries a critic's edge: calling a program piecemeal implies it lacks the coordinated design it needs. If the small steps follow a deliberate plan, the fairer word is incremental — that missing plan is the whole difference.

A decade later, a retired electrician rewired the lobby because he hated seeing it dark. The balcony rail came from a demolished church two counties away. No two jobs were coordinated. Workers matched nothing to a master drawing, because there was none; each crew solved the problem in front of it and left. Visitors today can read the whole history in the walls: six shades of brick, four styles of molding, wiring that loops around plaster nobody dared open.
