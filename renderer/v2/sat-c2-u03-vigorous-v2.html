What It Means

Vigorous means full of energy, force, or strength.

See the word in context

Two hours in, the town-hall debate stayed vigorous, hands still shooting up faster than the moderator could point.

At the fish market, vendor Sal hawks his catch with vigorous showmanship: hoisting whole tunas overhead, slapping ice from the crates, out-shouting three rival stalls before six a.m., then hosing down the stall himself at closing.

Vigorous describes the doer, not the task: it names energy, force, and strength in the action itself. A vigorous effort attacks the work; whether the work is hard is a separate question.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    energetic: Energetic says a person or action is full of active energy — always in motion, never idle. Vigorous adds force and strength to that energy: a vigorous scrub bears down, a vigorous debate pushes hard. An energetic child circles the yard all afternoon; a vigorous one wins the tug-of-war.

    spirited: Spirited points at verve — the flash of personality and heart inside an effort, like a spirited reply or a spirited pony. Vigorous measures the muscle behind the action rather than the personality in it. A spirited defense has heart; a vigorous defense has force.

    dynamic: Dynamic describes energy that drives change and keeps things moving — a dynamic speaker, a dynamic young company. Vigorous names the raw force and strength inside the action itself, whether or not anything changes. A dynamic firm keeps reinventing itself; a vigorous one simply outworks its rivals.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    feeble: weak and without force

    listless: drained of energy or interest

    Where it sits · how much muscle goes into it

    mild → lively → vigorous

    mild vs. vigorous: A mild workout barely raises your pulse; vigorous action puts real force and strength behind every motion.

    lively vs. vigorous: Lively means brisk and animated; vigorous adds muscle - a lively conversation sparkles, while a vigorous one is a full-contact sport.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral

    Vigorous praises the force in an action or person - exercise, debate, growth, scrubbing. Keep it apart from its unit-mate strenuous, which measures what a task demands rather than what a person brings: the workout is strenuous, the athlete is vigorous. For personality rather than force, lively or spirited is the lighter touch.
