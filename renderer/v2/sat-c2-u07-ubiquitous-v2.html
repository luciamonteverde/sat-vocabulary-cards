Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Ubiquitous means seeming to be everywhere at once — met wherever you go.

See the word in context

By August the summer's hit song was ubiquitous — dentist offices, gas pumps, three different neighbors' garages.

Ten years ago the scan-to-order code was a novelty at one downtown cafe, and the waiter had to teach every table how it worked. Now the little square is ubiquitous: parking meters, museum walls, church bulletins, the back of the ketchup bottle. Nobody teaches anybody anymore.

Ubiquitous is the right word because the code no longer belongs to any particular place — wherever you happen to stand, it has gotten there first. That everywhere-you-look quality, a little exaggerated and a little weary, is exactly what the word carries.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    omnipresent: present in all places at the same moment — the strict, literal, almost theological claim. Ubiquitous is the everyday exaggeration: it only insists that wherever you go, there the thing is.

    universal: shared by every member of a group without exception — universal describes who has something; ubiquitous describes where you keep meeting it. A universal habit belongs to everyone; a ubiquitous poster is simply on every wall.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    rare: met seldom; hard to find

    localized: found only in one particular place

    Where it sits · how impossible it is to go where it isn't

    widespread → ubiquitous → inescapable

    widespread vs. ubiquitous: Widespread is weaker than ubiquitous: a widespread fashion shows up in many places, but you can still spend a day without meeting it — ubiquitous means the day off doesn't exist.

    inescapable vs. ubiquitous: Inescapable is stronger than ubiquitous: the ubiquitous thing is wherever you look, but the inescapable one finds you even when you've stopped looking and are actively trying to avoid it.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral, often wry — tech writing, culture pieces, complaints

    Ubiquitous is slightly rueful by habit: writers reach for it about phones, logos, and songs that have saturated daily life. Its unit-mate pervasive works inside one whole (an attitude pervades an office); ubiquitous works across the map (the kiosk is in every airport). And mind universal, which claims every member without exception — a ubiquitous gadget is everywhere you look, not necessarily in every single hand.

    The acoustic ecologists spent three years hunting for silence, carrying recorders from desert canyons to old-growth forest to tundra far above the tree line. Human-made noise turned out to be ubiquitous: every site the team monitored picked it up, however many days of trail lay behind them. Jet traffic registered over the remotest valleys; generator and highway hum reached deep into designated wilderness; a station bedded in canyon rock logged engine noise before dawn. Loudness varied enormously — one site caught a single faint overflight a day, another a drone that never stopped — so the noise was hardly spread evenly. What the data ruled out was refuge: there was no longer anywhere the team could go and fail to record it.
