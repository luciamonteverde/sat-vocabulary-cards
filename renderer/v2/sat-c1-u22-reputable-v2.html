What It Means

Reputable means having a good, trustworthy reputation; respectable and legitimate.

See the word in context

Before hiring movers, Dad called three references to make sure the company was actually reputable.

Two listings offered the same guitar at the same price. The first seller had no reviews, a blurry photo, and wanted payment by gift card. The second had eight years on the platform, four hundred detailed reviews, a return policy, and answered Kira's questions within the hour. "Same guitar, but only one reputable seller," her brother said. "Pay the one with the track record."

The years of history, hundreds of reviews, clear policies, and prompt answers form the trustworthy track record that makes the second seller reputable — and the first one not.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    trustworthy: Trustworthy means deserving of confidence: this person or source will not cheat you or let you down. Reputable adds the public track record — not just trustworthy in fact but known to be, with reviews and references others can check. Trustworthiness lives in conduct; a reputation can be looked up.

    respectable: Respectable means meeting society's standards — proper, presentable, nothing anyone would raise an eyebrow at. Reputable is more practical: it says a business or source has a verified record of honest dealing. A shop can look perfectly respectable and still cheat you; reputable is about the record, not appearances.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    shady: of doubtful honesty

    fly-by-night: unreliable and likely to vanish

    Where it sits · how strong the good name is

    acceptable → reputable → distinguished

    acceptable vs. reputable: Acceptable means merely good enough; reputable means the good name has been checked and holds up — people vouch for it.

    distinguished vs. reputable: Distinguished goes beyond reputable: not just trustworthy, but standing out above its trustworthy peers.

      practical, transactional; for businesses, sources, and professionals

      Reputable is the coolest of this unit's respect words: it just means trustworthy and not shady — a reputable dealer, a reputable source — with none of the awe of revere or the earned grandeur of venerable; a two-year-old shop can be perfectly reputable, but only decades can make it venerable.
