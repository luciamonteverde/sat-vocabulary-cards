genial

/JEE-nee-ul/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Genial means warm, friendly, and good-humored in manner.

See the word in context

The genial innkeeper greeted every arriving guest with a broad smile and a cheerful joke about the mountain weather.

Customers at the corner barbershop rarely left in a bad mood. The barber, Mr. Ortiz, welcomed each person by name, asked about their families, and kept up a stream of gentle jokes while he worked. Even strangers who wandered in just to ask for directions got a warm greeting and a friendly wave on their way out. His genial manner made a ten-minute haircut feel like a visit with an old friend.

One person's warm, friendly, good-humored way of treating the people in front of him is exactly what genial describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    affable: Affable means friendly and easy to talk to - an approachable manner that puts people quickly at ease. It describes a person's social surface. Genial goes a shade warmer and sunnier: good humor that radiates outward, the joke arriving with the greeting. An affable clerk is easy company; a genial one leaves you smiling on the sidewalk.

    cordial: Cordial means friendly in a polite, formal way, and it thrives at arm's length - nations keep cordial relations, rivals exchange cordial letters. Genial is warmer and utterly personal: one person's good-humored manner toward the people in front of them. Cordial can be all protocol and no affection; genial is affection you can see on a face.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    surly: gruff and bad-tempered

    curt: rudely brief in speech

    Where it sits · how much warmth pours out

    polite → genial → effusive

    polite vs. genial: A polite host follows the rules of courtesy, while a genial one radiates real warmth and good humor toward each guest.

    effusive vs. genial: A genial greeting is warm and friendly, but an effusive one gushes with so much enthusiasm that it can overwhelm the person receiving it.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      everyday, warmly complimentary register

      Genial describes one person's manner, expression, or tone in the moment (a genial host, a genial smile, a genial chuckle). Save it for how an individual treats the people right in front of them, not for the general mood of a party or a place.
