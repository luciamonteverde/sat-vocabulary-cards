What It Means

Loquacious means very talkative.

See the word in context

Our loquacious cousin can spin twenty easy minutes of talk out of a walk to the mailbox, and somehow you listen.

The museum pairs each new docent with Mrs. Abebe, its most loquacious veteran, on the theory that fluency is contagious: she narrates a single amphora through three dynasties without once checking her notes.

Loquacious means very talkative — words in easy, abundant supply. Unlike its sour cousin garrulous, it doesn't accuse the talker of boring anyone: a loquacious guest can be the life of the table. The word measures the flow, not the tedium.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    chatty: Chatty is light, sociable talk — friendly small exchanges over a fence or between errands. Loquacious describes real volume: talk in easy, tumbling abundance, an hour where a sentence would do. A chatty neighbor passes the time of day; a loquacious one narrates the whole afternoon.

    wordy: Wordy faults the writing, not the writer: more words on the page than the idea needs, the kind an editor trims. Loquacious describes a person's abundant flow of talk, and it passes no judgment — a loquacious host can be a delight. Reports are wordy; people are loquacious.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    quiet: simply not talking much

    tongue-tied: unable to get the words out at all

    Where it sits · the sheer abundance of speech

    conversational → talkative → loquacious

    conversational vs. loquacious: Conversational is the base rung, far below loquacious — it just means able to hold up an ordinary exchange.

    talkative vs. loquacious: Talkative comes closer but stops short of loquacious — a talkative friend talks a lot, while a loquacious one talks in tumbling, unbroken abundance.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral / bookish

      Loquacious measures the volume of talk without passing judgment: a loquacious host may be a delight. Garrulous (also in this unit) is the same volume gone rancid — tedious, trivial, endless. Praise or gently tease with loquacious; complain with garrulous.

He is famously loquacious — a man who can carry a quarter hour on the subject of one interesting cloud — yet the corner never feels delayed, only entertained.
