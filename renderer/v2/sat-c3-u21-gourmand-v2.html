Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Gourmand means someone fond of eating and drinking.

See the word in context

A gourmand to his bones, my cousin plans road trips around diners the way other people plan them around monuments.

The food festival's organizers know their audience: gourmands who arrive at the opening bell with folding chairs, sample every stall twice, and compare notes on the brisket like scholars at a conference.

Gourmand is warmer than 'glutton' and humbler than 'gourmet' — a glutton eats past all pleasure, and a gourmet prizes refinement, while a gourmand simply loves eating and drinking, enthusiastically and in quantity.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    epicure: An epicure is a connoisseur of subtle pleasures, food above all — delight lies in discerning the quality most people miss. A gourmand's delight lies in the eating itself: appetite, abundance, and the joy of the table. The epicure hunts the perfect bite; the gourmand cheerfully finishes the platter.

    gourmet: A gourmet is a judge of food: refinement, technique, and quality are the whole point, and quantity is beside it. A gourmand is the eater rather than the judge — hearty love of eating and drinking itself, enthusiastically and in volume. The gourmet savors one perfect course; the gourmand happily orders seconds.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    abstainer: one who deliberately goes without indulgence

    nibbler: one who barely touches the plate

    Where it sits · how far real appetite runs

    foodie → gourmand → glutton

    foodie vs. gourmand: Foodie stops short of gourmand — a foodie photographs the tasting menu, while a gourmand orders it twice and means it.

    glutton vs. gourmand: Glutton goes further than gourmand — the gourmand eats for joy and stops at contentment's far edge; the glutton eats past pleasure into plain excess.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      playful / warm

      Gourmand carries a wink — appetite as personality. Gourmet praises taste; glutton condemns excess; gourmand sits between them, fond and a little indulgent. Use it for the friend who plans the whole vacation around lunch.
