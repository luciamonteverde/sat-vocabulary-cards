audacious

/aw-DAY-shuhs/ - rhymes with gracious

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Audacious means boldly daring; willing to take surprising risks.

See the word in context

The intern made the audacious suggestion that the firm scrap its flagship product and rebuild it from scratch - and the partners, to everyone's surprise, agreed.

The young architect's audacious competition entry proposed hanging the concert hall off a cliff face above the river. Rivals submitted safe glass boxes; the jury, half scandalized and half thrilled, chose the cliff.

Audacious sits between brave and reckless: it names boldness that startles - risks most people would not dare - but unlike recklessness it does not imply carelessness. An audacious plan can be carefully calculated; it simply refuses to play safe.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    fearless: Fearless describes what someone feels - no fear at all - whether or not any risk is taken. Audacious describes what someone does: a move so boldly daring it startles onlookers. A fearless firefighter may follow every rule; an audacious one tries the entry nobody has dared.

    bold: Bold is the everyday word for confident and brave, covering everything from a bold answer in class to a bold choice of color. Audacious raises the stakes: boldness surprising enough to make onlookers gasp, a risk most people would never take.

    brazen: Brazen is boldness with the shame removed - acting openly where most people would at least blush - and the word usually disapproves. Audacious keeps the surprise but not the verdict: an audacious plan startles, and may be admired or condemned for it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    timid: shrinking from any risk

    faint-hearted: lacking the nerve to act

    Where it sits · how far boldness outruns caution

    daring → audacious → reckless

    daring vs. audacious: A daring move takes real nerve; audacious goes further - boldness so surprising it makes onlookers gasp.

    reckless vs. audacious: Reckless pushes past audacious and loses the admiration: the audacious climber takes a shocking but calculated line, while the reckless one ignores the weather entirely.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral, often admiring

      Audacious carries a gasp - boldness that startles, sometimes admired, sometimes disapproved of. Plain 'bold' or 'daring' praises without the shock. Keep it apart from its ladder-mate reckless, which condemns carelessness outright. And note the split with its unit-mate visionary: an audacious plan is daring in the doing, while a visionary one is daring in what it imagines the future could become.

Entry 47 was audacious even by the jury's later account: a roof of tilted concrete sails, a design its own engineers initially called unbuildable. Three jurors wanted it disqualified. Today the building appears on the nation's currency, a risk nobody had dared before and nobody has matched since.
