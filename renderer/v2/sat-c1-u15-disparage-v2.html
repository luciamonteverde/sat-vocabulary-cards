disparage

/dis-PAIR-ij/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Disparage means to criticize or speak ill of someone or something; to belittle or put it down.

See the word in context

The head chef would often disparage frozen vegetables, insisting that no serious kitchen should ever rely on them.

At the community garden, Rosa listened politely as a longtime member continued to disparage store-bought compost, calling it lifeless dirt in a fancy bag. Rosa had used it successfully for years, so she simply smiled and kept planting.

The longtime member is not offering helpful advice; he is belittling the compost and dismissing it as worthless, which is exactly what it means to disparage something.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    belittle: To belittle is to make something seem smaller and less important than it really is - waving off a hard-won achievement as easy. To disparage is the broader formal word for talking something down, and belittling is its favorite method. Every belittling remark disparages; disparage also covers sneers, put-downs, and dismissals of every kind.

    demean: To demean is to attack dignity - to treat a person as beneath respect, lowering their standing. To disparage is to talk down the worth of things as readily as people: a chef can disparage frozen vegetables all day. You demean only people; you can disparage a person, a method, or a product.

    ridicule: To ridicule is to make someone or something an object of mocking laughter - the weapon is the joke. To disparage needs no humor at all: a flat dismissal of something as worthless disparages without a single laugh. Ridicule wants an audience laughing; disparaging just wants the target diminished.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    praise: to express approval or admiration

    compliment: to say something kind or admiring

    Where it sits · how harshly the words tear someone down

    criticize → disparage → vilify

    criticize vs. disparage: To criticize is simply to point out faults, but to disparage adds a dismissive, belittling tone that treats the target as unworthy of respect.

    vilify vs. disparage: To disparage is to belittle, while to vilify goes much further and paints someone as truly evil or hateful.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      Formal / critical

      Disparage is a formal word for belittling criticism. Use it when someone talks down about a person or thing, not when they simply disagree; you can disagree respectfully, but you cannot disparage respectfully.
