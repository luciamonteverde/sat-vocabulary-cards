mitigation

/mit-uh-GAY-shun/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Mitigation means the action of reducing how severe, serious, or harmful something is.

See the word in context

The county's flood mitigation program restored wetlands upstream, so storms still bring high water, but far less of it ever reaches people's homes.

Homes near the airport received thicker windows and insulated walls as part of a noise mitigation project. The planes still fly overhead all day, but the roar inside the houses is far quieter now.

Notice that the project does not stop the planes or the noise at its source; it only reduces how harsh the noise is for the people affected. Reducing the severity of a harm without eliminating it is precisely what mitigation means.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    alleviation: Alleviation is relief given to sufferers — it lessens pain or trouble that people are already feeling, as aid alleviates hunger. Mitigation works one step earlier, on the harm itself: it reduces how severe or damaging something is. You alleviate the suffering; you mitigate what causes it.

    reduction: Reduction is the all-purpose word for making any amount or degree smaller — a reduction in price, noise, or staff. It is neutral about what shrinks. Mitigation is specifically about harm: a deliberate effort to make something less severe, serious, or damaging, without claiming to end it.

    softening: Softening means making something land less harshly — you soften a blow, criticism, or bad news, often just by changing tone or presentation. Mitigation reduces the harm itself, not merely how it feels, and it is the standard term for organized measures like flood mitigation.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    aggravation: making something worse

    escalation: increase in intensity or seriousness

    Where it sits · how much of the problem is removed

    easing → mitigation → elimination

    easing vs. mitigation: Easing is a gentle, partial lessening of discomfort, while mitigation is a deliberate effort to reduce real harm or severity.

    elimination vs. mitigation: Mitigation reduces how bad a problem's effects are, while elimination removes the problem completely, which mitigation never promises.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      Formal / legal and policy

      A staple of policy and legal writing, as in risk mitigation and mitigation measures. Mitigation only reduces harm or severity; it never claims to cure or eliminate the problem, which is what separates it from words like elimination or remedy.
