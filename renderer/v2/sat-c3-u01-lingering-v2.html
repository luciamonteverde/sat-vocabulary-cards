lingering

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Lingering means lasting longer than expected and slow to fade or leave.

See the word in context

A lingering frost kept the garden gray into May, and the tulips finally came up looking apologetic.

When the stitches came out, the surgeon warned Maya about a lingering stiffness in the wrist — nothing dangerous, just slow to leave. She noticed it all summer; by October it visited only on cold mornings, a fainter guest each week.

The stiffness is neither permanent nor gone. It stays past the point where she expected it to end and fades by degrees, and that unhurried overstaying is what lingering marks: the word looks at something on its way out and notices how slowly the exit happens.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    persistent: Persistent means refusing to leave — it digs in and outlasts every effort to be rid of it. Lingering is gentler: the thing is going, just slowly, and nobody has to fight it.

    residual: Residual names what is left over once the rest is gone — a fixed trace, measured after the fact. Lingering describes the leaving itself: something still mid-fade, present past its expected end.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    fleeting: gone almost as soon as it arrives

    hasty: done in a rush, without staying

    Where it sits · how stubbornly it stays

    residual → lingering → persistent

    residual vs. lingering: Residual is milder than lingering: a residual ache is the faint trace left after the injury is essentially over, while a lingering ache is still actively hanging on, taking its time to leave.

    persistent vs. lingering: Persistent is stronger than lingering: a lingering cough is slow to fade on its own, while a persistent one digs in and refuses to leave no matter what the doctor throws at it.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / reflective

    Lingering watches an exit happen slowly, and it takes whatever tone its noun gives it — a lingering perfume is wistful, a lingering doubt is unwelcome. Inside this unit, keep the lanes: belated is about arriving after the proper time, lingering about leaving after it; and where an evanescent thing is gone before you can name it, a lingering one is still here after it should have gone.
