Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Belated means coming later than it should have.

See the word in context

Marco's belated birthday card reached his sister in March, complete with an apology and a drawing of a very slow snail.

The landlord's belated repairs began the week the tenants' lawyer finally wrote to him: the furnace fixed in April that had failed in November, the leak patched after the ceiling stain had grown a coastline. Everything on the list got done — and every item carried the same silent postscript: too late to matter as it should have.

Belated means coming later than it should have, and the should is the sting. A slow train is merely delayed; a belated repair, apology, or thank-you arrives after the moment it was owed has mostly passed. The thing still counts — winter, however, is over.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    delayed: Delayed means held up: an obstacle — weather, a backlog, a slow train — got in the way, and the thing is still coming. A belated gesture met no obstacle at all; it simply arrived after the moment it was owed had passed. Delayed blames the route; belated admits the lateness.

    unpunctual: Unpunctual describes a person and a habit — someone routinely not on time, with no single occasion in view. Belated attaches to the gesture itself: the card, the tribute, the apology that arrives after its moment has passed. An unpunctual friend sends many belated birthday wishes.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    timely: arriving at just the right moment

    prompt: done at once, without delay

    Where it sits · how far past its proper time

    tardy → overdue → belated

    tardy vs. belated: Tardy is the mildest rung — a tardy student misses five minutes of class; belated arrivals are measured in seasons and years.

    overdue vs. belated: Overdue stops short of belated: an overdue book is past its date but still expected back, while a belated tribute turns up after everyone has stopped waiting.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral / faintly rueful

      Belated is the word for gestures that arrive after their moment — apologies, congratulations, recognitions, repairs. It carries a small bow of acknowledged fault, which is why greeting cards say "happy belated birthday" and not "happy tardy birthday." Keep tardy for everyday small lateness and overdue for deadlines still in force; belated is for the moment already gone.
