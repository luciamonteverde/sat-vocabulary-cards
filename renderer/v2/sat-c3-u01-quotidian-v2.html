What It Means

Quotidian means occurring every day and unremarkable for that reason.

See the word in context

Retirement, he warned us, strips the quotidian scaffolding from a day — no train to catch, no desk to reach by nine.

The director's admirers praise exactly what her critics mock: whole minutes spent on quotidian business — a kettle filled, a coat buttoned, a bus caught in the rain. She cuts none of it. Her reasoning is that when the phone call that changes everything finally comes, it must interrupt a life the audience has actually lived in, not a highlight reel.

Quotidian is the precise choice because it means occurring every day and unremarkable for that reason — the kettle matters because it is filled every morning, not despite it. And unlike words that sneer, quotidian can honor: in her films the daily round is the texture of a life, not the boring part between events.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    everyday: Everyday is the plain-register twin of quotidian: both mark what a person meets daily. But everyday simply labels the category and passes no judgment on the sameness, while quotidian invites you to notice dailiness itself — in a seminar or a poem, often with respect.

    workaday: Workaday describes what is built for plain, practical use — workaday boots, workaday prose that gets the job done. Quotidian is about recurrence instead: a thing is quotidian because it happens every day, whether or not it is practical, and the word can carry real respect.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    extraordinary: far outside the usual run of things

    exceptional: rare enough to stand out on its own

    Where it sits · how deeply the sameness soaks in

    routine → quotidian → humdrum

    routine vs. quotidian: Routine is the milder marker — a routine check is simply the standard one; quotidian folds the task into the very texture of every day.

    humdrum vs. quotidian: Humdrum goes further than quotidian: quotidian says a thing belongs to every day, while humdrum adds that the sameness has gone dull and gray.

    Apply

    Word Choice

    literary / intellectual

    Quotidian is the seminar-room word for the everyday, and it can carry respect — a poet's quotidian subjects are a choice, not a failure. Its unit-mate mundane (in this unit's word list) shrugs where quotidian observes, and humdrum openly yawns. Reach for quotidian when dailiness itself is the point; reach for mundane when you only mean plain.

    For one year, the photographer Ana Reyes shot a single frame every morning: her kitchen table, seven o'clock, whatever happened to be on it. Critics who expected drama were puzzled. The images are quotidian by design -- a folded newspaper, a jar of pencils, toast. Seen one at a time, they are almost nothing. Seen as a wall of 365 prints, they become a calendar of a life. A mug migrates. Pill bottles arrive in March and multiply. The newspaper stops coming in June. Reyes says the project taught her that the repeated day is not the enemy of meaning but its container -- you only see a life change by watching what it does every morning.
