pinnacle

/PIN-uh-kuhl/ - the stress falls on PIN

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Pinnacle means the highest point of success or achievement.

See the word in context

Being asked to design the city's new concert hall was the pinnacle of the architect's forty-year career.

The violinist had played a thousand stages, but walking out alone at the great Vienna hall — the concert every teacher had described in hushed tones since she was six — she knew she had reached the pinnacle: nothing in her art stood higher.

Pinnacle names the single topmost point, not a stretch of good years: a career holds many fine moments, but only one pinnacle — the achievement above which there is nothing left to climb.

    Accuracy

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    peak: Peak is the everyday word for a top — mountains, careers, afternoon traffic — and peaks can recur: a career may peak twice. Pinnacle is grander and admits no repeat: the single crowning achievement above which nothing stands. Every pinnacle is a peak; only one peak can be the pinnacle.

    apex: Apex is the geometry word: the highest point of a triangle, an arc, or a sales curve, stated coolly and without applause. Pinnacle carries the applause — it crowns a career or an art with a sense of glory earned. An apex is measured; a pinnacle is celebrated.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    trough: the lowest dip in a curve of fortunes

    valley: the low ground lying between two heights

    Where it sits · how near the very top it stands

    milestone → triumph → pinnacle

    milestone vs. pinnacle: A milestone only marks distance covered — a first job, a hundredth show — with the road still running on ahead; a pinnacle is where the road stops climbing.

    triumph vs. pinnacle: A triumph is one great victory among the many a career may hold; a pinnacle sits above them all — the single highest achievement, with nothing left to climb beyond it.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      admiring / slightly formal

      Pinnacle crowns a career or field: the pinnacle of her profession, of the sport, of the art. Its unit-mate zenith prefers eras and powers at their height — an empire's zenith — while pinnacle prefers a single crowning achievement. Both allow only one: if there were two, neither would be the pinnacle.
