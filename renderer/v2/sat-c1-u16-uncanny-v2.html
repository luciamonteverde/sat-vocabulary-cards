uncanny

/un-KAN-ee/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Uncanny means strange in a way that seems to defy natural explanation, with no ordinary cause that can account for it.

See the word in context

There was something uncanny about the antique doll in the shop window; no matter where visitors stood, its painted eyes seemed to follow them across the room.

Long after the twins had been assigned to cabins on opposite ends of the lake, their camp counselors kept noticing something uncanny: the girls would start humming the same tune at the same moment, as if some invisible signal passed between them across the water.

The twins' perfectly matched behavior feels mysterious and a little unsettling, beyond ordinary coincidence, which is what makes it uncanny.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    eerie: Eerie describes strangeness felt as fear: a ghostly, frightening atmosphere, whatever its cause. Uncanny adds a stricter test — the strangeness seems to defy natural explanation, the way a guess that proves impossibly exact does. Something eerie scares you; something uncanny also puzzles you.

    unnerving: Unnerving means making someone lose their calm, and anything can do it: a near miss, a long stare, a sudden drop. Uncanny points at one particular source of unease — a strangeness that seems to slip past natural explanation altogether.

    otherworldly: Otherworldly describes what seems to belong beyond the natural world entirely, like light or music too perfect for earth. Uncanny stays in this world: an uncanny event happens among ordinary things, yet resists every ordinary explanation you try on it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    ordinary: normal and unremarkable

    natural: easily explained by everyday causes

    Where it sits · how far beyond explanation it lies

    odd → uncanny → supernatural

    odd vs. uncanny: Something odd is merely a bit strange, while something uncanny is strange in a way that raises the hairs on the back of your neck.

    supernatural vs. uncanny: An uncanny event feels almost beyond explanation, but a supernatural one openly breaks the laws of nature.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral

    Uncanny describes strangeness with an unsettling, almost supernatural edge, as in 'an uncanny resemblance' or 'an uncanny ability.' Save it for things that give you a small shiver; something that is merely different or quirky is not uncanny.
