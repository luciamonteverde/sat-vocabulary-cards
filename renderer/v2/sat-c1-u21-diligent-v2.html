diligent

/DIL-ih-jent/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Diligent means showing steady, careful effort in one's work.

See the word in context

The most diligent volunteer showed up every Saturday for two years, rain or shine.

Nobody noticed Priya's routine at first. She arrived at the community garden twenty minutes early each week, watered on a strict rotation, and logged what she planted in a small notebook. When aphids appeared, she researched remedies that evening and applied them the next morning. Season after season she kept it up without being asked or reminded. Three years later, hers was the only plot that had never once failed — the quiet reward of diligent work.

Priya's steady, careful effort maintained week after week for years — early arrivals, strict rotations, prompt problem-solving — is exactly what makes her diligent.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    industrious: Industrious means hardworking by habit - an industrious person is always busy, always producing, filling the hours with output. Diligent adds care to the effort: the work is not only steady but attentive, done properly with nothing skipped. An industrious worker impresses by how much gets done; a diligent one by how carefully.

    hardworking: Hardworking means putting in consistent effort - long hours, real exertion, no slacking. Diligent describes the quality of that effort as much as the quantity: steady work carried out carefully, again and again, over time. A hardworking student puts in the hours; a diligent one also checks every answer before handing the test in.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    lazy: avoiding work

    negligent: failing to give proper care

    Where it sits · how much steady effort goes in

    dutiful → diligent → tireless

    dutiful vs. diligent: Dutiful is weaker — a dutiful worker does what is required and no more; a diligent one brings steady, careful effort that goes beyond mere obligation.

    tireless vs. diligent: Tireless is stronger than diligent: it suggests effort that never flags at all, day or night, while diligence is sustained but human-paced.

    neutral, admiring; report-card and workplace staple

    Diligent praises steady, ongoing effort across time — showing up and doing careful work again and again. Its close cousin in this unit, painstaking, praises something narrower: the intense care poured into the fine details of one particular task.
