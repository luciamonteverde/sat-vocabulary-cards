bureaucratic

/byoor-uh-KRAT-ik/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Bureaucratic means tangled in excessive administrative rules, procedures, and red tape.

See the word in context

Renewing a simple permit became a bureaucratic slog of forms, stamps, and waiting rooms.

Amara only wanted to hang a small mural on the community center wall. Instead she hit a bureaucratic maze: Form 7B from the parks office, which required a signature from the arts council, which would not sign until the safety office issued a paint clearance. Six weeks, four offices, and eleven documents later, she finally received permission to do what would take a single afternoon with a brush.

The process is bureaucratic because layers of administrative rules and paperwork swallow what should be a simple task.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    procedural: Procedural means bound to official steps and formalities, and it is a neutral word: a procedural step may be perfectly sensible. Bureaucratic is the complaint version: the offices, forms, and approvals have multiplied far past what the task needs. A process can be procedural without bothering anyone; it turns bureaucratic when the red tape takes over.

    rule-bound: Rule-bound means controlled by rigid regulations, with little freedom to bend or make exceptions. Bureaucratic adds the machinery and the frustration: layers of offices, forms, and approvals that swallow simple tasks. A rule-bound umpire just enforces the rulebook strictly; a bureaucratic agency makes you stand in three lines to file one page.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    streamlined: simplified to work quickly and smoothly

    efficient: achieving results without wasted effort

    Where it sits · how hard the process is to navigate

    formal → bureaucratic → labyrinthine

    formal vs. bureaucratic: A formal process follows set rules, while a bureaucratic one buries you in forms, offices, and approvals.

    labyrinthine vs. bureaucratic: A bureaucratic system is tangled in red tape, while a labyrinthine one is so maze-like you may never find your way through at all.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      mildly critical, everyday register

      Bureaucratic almost always carries a note of frustration: it describes systems bogged down in forms, approvals, and procedures. Calling an office bureaucratic is a mild criticism, so avoid the word when you simply mean organized or careful.
