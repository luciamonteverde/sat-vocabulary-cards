divulge

/dih-VULJ/ - rhymes with bulge

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Divulge means to make something secret known to others.

See the word in context

Jury members are warned never to divulge what was said in the deliberation room, even years after the verdict.

The mapmaker knew the one pass through the ridge and would not divulge it; three expeditions offered him a season's wages, and he drew them the long way around instead.

Divulge always involves a secret crossing a boundary. To reveal is neutral — a curtain can reveal a stage — and to hint is to let a corner show. To divulge is to hand the secret itself to someone outside the circle, deliberately and in full.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    reveal: To reveal is simply to make something visible or known, and no secret is required — a curtain can reveal a stage. To divulge is narrower: a genuine secret crosses a boundary, handed deliberately to someone outside the circle. Reveal is neutral; divulge accuses.

    let slip: To let slip is to release a secret by accident — a name dropped mid-story before the speaker can catch it. Divulging is the deliberate act: the teller chooses to hand the secret over. Both cross the line, but only divulge does it on purpose.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    bottle up: keeps a matter unspoken inside

    hush up: actively keeps a matter quiet

    Where it sits · how fully the secret gets out

    hint → divulge → trumpet

    hint vs. divulge: Hint is milder than divulge — a hint lets one corner of the secret show and makes the listener do the rest of the work.

    trumpet vs. divulge: Trumpet goes further than divulge: to divulge is to pass the secret to someone, while to trumpet it is to blast it to everyone in earshot.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / careful

    Divulge belongs to contracts, courtrooms, and confidences — wherever the telling is a breach. Reveal is the everyday neutral word; leak adds wrongdoing through a side door; trumpet adds volume. Divulge insists on one thing: what crossed the line was a secret.
