Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Affable means friendly, warm, and easy to talk to.

See the word in context

Our affable mail carrier lingers at every gate to chat, and even the crankiest houses on the route wave him over.

The bookshop hired two clerks the same week: one knows every title in stock, and one is simply affable — greeting regulars by name, asking after their dogs, and turning a quick purchase into a pleasant five-minute visit. The line forms, happily, at the second register.

Affable is more precise than 'nice' or 'friendly' because it names a particular kind of warmth: being easy to approach and easy to talk to. A shy person can be deeply kind without being affable — affability lives in relaxed, welcoming conversation.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    warm: Warm names general kindness and care — a warm coach, a warm note — without saying anything about how conversation flows. Affable is narrower: friendliness that shows up specifically in easy, welcoming talk. A warm person may be shy and quiet; an affable one has you chatting within a minute.

    personable: Personable means pleasing in manner and appearance overall — a good first impression from across the room. Affable pins the pleasantness to conversation itself: easy to approach and easy to talk with. A personable candidate looks and carries herself well; an affable one makes the interview feel like a chat.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    gruff: rough and short in manner, the opposite of welcoming talk

    standoffish: cold and distant, keeping people at arm's length

    Where it sits · how much warmth the manner shows

    approachable → affable → gushing

    approachable vs. affable: Approachable is milder than affable — an approachable manager won't bite if you knock, but an affable one pulls up a chair and has you talking in a minute.

    gushing vs. affable: Gushing goes further than affable and overshoots it — a gushing host buries you in compliments until you squirm, while affable warmth keeps the conversation easy.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / warmly descriptive

    Reach for affable when the warmth shows up in conversation — someone easy to approach and easy to talk to. Amiable (also in this unit) praises a person's overall good nature, and gregarious names a love of company itself; affable is specifically the talk-to-me quality of an interviewer, a host, a mentor.
