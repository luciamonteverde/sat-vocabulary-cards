deliberation

/dih-LIB-uh-RAY-shun/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Deliberation means long and careful consideration or discussion before reaching a decision.

See the word in context

Only after months of deliberation did the scholarship committee narrow two hundred applications down to a single winner.

After two full days of deliberation, during which the jurors re-read the transcripts and argued over every piece of testimony, the jury finally returned its verdict.

Two full days of re-reading transcripts and arguing over testimony before deciding is exactly the long, careful weighing of a decision that deliberation describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    contemplation: Contemplation is quiet, often solitary deep thought — you can contemplate a painting or the stars with no decision anywhere in view. Deliberation is aimed: careful weighing, often through discussion, in order to decide something. Contemplation may wander wherever it likes; deliberation works toward a verdict.

    consideration: Consideration is a general weighing of factors — lighter and quicker, as in 'after some consideration.' Deliberation is longer, more careful, and often collective: the way a jury or committee talks a decision through. A moment's thought is consideration; days of weighing and debate are deliberation.

    forethought: Forethought is planning done ahead of time, before a situation even arrives — packing a spare tire shows forethought. Deliberation happens once the decision is actually in front of you: sustained weighing of real options. Forethought looks ahead to what might come; deliberation digs into the choice at hand.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    impulsiveness: acting on sudden urges without thinking

    haste: hurried action that skips careful thought

    Where it sits · how sustained and purposeful the weighing is

    thought → reflection → deliberation

    thought vs. deliberation: A passing thought weighs a choice for only a moment, while deliberation examines it from every side.

    reflection vs. deliberation: Reflection is quiet thinking about something, but deliberation is a focused, sustained weighing of options before a decision.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal

    The related adjective 'deliberate' (done on purpose) and verb 'deliberate' (to weigh options) share the root but shift meaning; deliberation always names the careful weighing process itself. The plural 'deliberations' often refers to a jury's or committee's closed-door discussions.
