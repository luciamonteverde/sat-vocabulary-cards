surfeit

/SUR-fit/ - the stress falls on SUR

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Surfeit means an overabundant supply — too much of something, to the point of harm.

See the word in context

A surfeit of trophies from the participation era filled four boxes that no one would ever unpack.

The aid warehouse learned that a surfeit can paralyze as surely as a shortage: donated coats arrived by the ton after the telethon, and sorting them swallowed the volunteers' whole winter.

Surfeit is the abundance word with a stomachache built in — from an Old French verb meaning 'overdo.' A surplus sits harmlessly in storage; a surfeit has already been forced on someone, and the system — a market, a schedule, a digestion — is suffering for it.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    surplus: A surplus is the extra left over once needs are met — grain in the silo, budget carried forward — and it sits harmlessly in storage, often welcome. A surfeit is oversupply that has turned on its owner: whatever holds it, a market or a stomach, is already suffering.

    overindulgence: Overindulgence is too much taken by choice — the fourth dessert, the extra hour of scrolling — excess a person consumes. Surfeit covers the whole territory of harmful too-much, including oversupply nobody chose: a surfeit can hurt a seller who never consumed a thing.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    deficit: the measurable gap of too little

    moderation: the habit of stopping at enough

    Where it sits · how harmful the overshoot becomes

    sufficiency → excess → surfeit

    sufficiency vs. surfeit: Sufficiency stops short of surfeit — a sufficiency is exactly enough, the point where a wise eater puts down the fork.

    excess vs. surfeit: Excess stops short of surfeit — excess is more than needed, while a surfeit is excess arrived at the point of harm, the extra course you regret.
