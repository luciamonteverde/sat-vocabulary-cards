Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Conformist means a person who behaves the same way as everyone around them.

See the word in context

A conformist to the last, Deng studied the other diners before ordering exactly what the next table had.

Every station in the market row sells the same three phone cases now. Rivera stocked hand-lettered ones for a month, watched two neighboring vendors smirk, and quietly turned conformist, going back to the bestseller list everyone else orders from. Nothing forced the change — no fine, no rule. The stalls on either side simply never varied, and in the end neither did he.

Conformist is the exact word for Rivera, because a conformist is a person who behaves the same way as everyone around them — the pull is the crowd itself, not any official rule. Follower is looser: a follower trails a leader or a trend and may still keep private tastes; the conformist's tastes surrender too.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    copycat: A copycat imitates one particular person — the same jokes, the same handwriting, traceable to a single model. A conformist has no single model: he matches the whole crowd around him, and the group's sameness, not any one person's example, is what his choices copy.

    yes-man: A yes-man agrees with whoever holds power — the boss floats a weak idea and hears only yes. A conformist needs no boss: the pressure comes sideways, from everybody at once, and it is the crowd's habits, not anyone's orders, that a conformist's choices dissolve into.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    individualist: sets their own course and wears the difference openly

    rebel: pushes against the crowd on purpose

    Where it sits · how fully independent choice is surrendered

    follower → conformist → sheep

    follower vs. conformist: A follower surrenders less than a conformist — a follower falls in behind one leader or fashion and may still keep a private opinion or two, while the conformist's own tastes surrender along with everything else.

    sheep vs. conformist: Sheep goes further than conformist — the conformist still chooses to match the crowd, while calling people sheep says the choosing has stopped altogether.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      mildly critical

      Conformist carries a raised eyebrow: it names someone whose choices dissolve into the crowd's. Keep it apart from its unit-mates — compliant means meeting written rules, and obedience answers a person in authority; the conformist obeys nobody in particular, only the pressure of everybody. A traditionalist, on the other hand, holds to the past even when the crowd moves on.
