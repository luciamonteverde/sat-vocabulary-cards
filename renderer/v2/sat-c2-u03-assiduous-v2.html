Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Assiduous means showing persistent care and hard work.

See the word in context

The museum's assiduous registrar numbered and photographed all twelve thousand artifacts, never letting a single crate go unlogged.

Marco, an assiduous apprentice electrician, traced every circuit twice, kept a notebook of every fault he had ever seen, and re-checked his own finished work before the inspector could. By his third year, journeymen were phoning him with questions.

Assiduous goes beyond 'hardworking': it names care that never lets up - steady, watchful attention to every detail, day after day. A hardworking person puts in the hours; an assiduous one also makes sure nothing slips through them.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    industrious: Industrious praises someone who works hard and stays busy - plenty of hours, plenty of output. Assiduous narrows the praise: not just hard work but persistent, careful attention, so that nothing slips through. You can be industrious and still miss details; the assiduous worker never lets them slip.

    thorough: Thorough means complete and careful on a single task - every corner checked before the job is called done. Assiduous stretches that care across time, tending the details day after day. One well-finished report is thorough; a decade of them, none ever skipped, is assiduous.

    meticulous: Meticulous care is about precision in the moment - every measurement exact, every detail checked twice. Assiduous care adds time: the same watchful effort kept up day after day, month after month. A meticulous editor perfects one manuscript; an assiduous one guards every issue, year after year.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    careless: letting details slip through

    slack: loose and lazy about duties

    Where it sits · how relentlessly the care is kept up

    attentive → assiduous → obsessive

    attentive vs. assiduous: An attentive clerk notices what is in front of her; assiduous adds persistence - checking and rechecking, day after day, until nothing slips.

    obsessive vs. assiduous: Obsessive pushes past assiduous into unhealthy territory: the assiduous bookkeeper double-checks the ledger and goes home, while the obsessive one is still re-adding columns at midnight that already balance.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      formal, admiring

      Assiduous praises steady, careful effort applied over time - the register is admiring and slightly formal. Its unit-mates pull in other directions: resolute is about an unshakable decision, and zealous about burning enthusiasm; assiduous is quieter, the virtue of someone who simply never stops tending the details. In casual speech, 'hardworking' or 'careful' is the plainer choice.
