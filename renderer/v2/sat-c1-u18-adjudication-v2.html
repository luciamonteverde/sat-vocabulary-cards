adjudication

/uh-joo-dih-KAY-shun/

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Adjudication means the formal process by which a court, judge, or official body hears a dispute and issues a binding decision.

See the word in context

The property-line quarrel finally went to adjudication after three years of angry letters had settled nothing.

Two rival game studios each claimed to own the rights to the same character design. After months of failed negotiation, the dispute moved to formal adjudication before a federal judge. Both sides submitted evidence, expert witnesses testified, and the judge issued a binding ruling that settled the question of ownership. However the parties felt about the outcome, the adjudication gave them a final answer.

A dispute was formally decided by a judge through an official, binding process, which is precisely what adjudication is.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    determination: A determination is an official decision reached after formal consideration — an agency determines eligibility, a board determines an award. Adjudication names the whole hearing-and-deciding process rather than its final product: evidence is heard, both sides argue, and only then does the binding decision issue. A determination is the outcome; adjudication is the process that produces it.

    ruling: A ruling is the official decision itself — the answer a judge or authority hands down at the end. Adjudication is the entire formal proceeding that leads there: the hearing of evidence, the weighing of arguments, and finally the binding decision. The ruling is one moment; the adjudication is the whole proceeding.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    deadlock: a standstill in which no decision can be reached

    Where it sits · how far official power steps in

    mediation → adjudication → sentencing

    mediation vs. adjudication: Mediation only helps two sides talk their way toward their own agreement, while adjudication ends with an official, binding decision.

    sentencing vs. adjudication: Adjudication settles who is right in a dispute, and sentencing goes a step further by imposing an actual punishment after that judgment.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal, legal register

    Adjudication typically pairs with disputes, claims, and cases (the adjudication of a benefits claim). Use it for the official deciding process itself, not for casual opinions or informal advice.
