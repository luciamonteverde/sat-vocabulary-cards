quixotic

/kwik-SOT-ik/ - rhymes with exotic

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Quixotic means idealistic in an impractical, unrealistic way.

See the word in context

Everyone loves Grandpa Milo's quixotic pledge to translate the town's entire archive into Esperanto; the archive gains a shelf a week, and Grandpa is eighty-one.

His quixotic project to reunite every lost glove in the city — he kept a warehouse of left hands waiting for their partners — charmed the newspapers and emptied his savings account twice.

Quixotic is more precise than 'unrealistic' because it adds nobility and romance to the doomed arithmetic: a quixotic plan charges at something huge and worthy with equipment that cannot possibly win. We smile at quixotic people even while wincing at their budgets.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    utopian: Utopian dreams a perfected world on paper — ideal societies, flawless systems, no rough edges. Quixotic shares the idealism but saddles up and charges at the real world, odds and all. The utopian writes the blueprint; the quixotic soul rides at the actual windmill with whatever is in the shed.

    impractical: Impractical means it won't work, plainly stated — a verdict with no affection in it. Quixotic delivers the same verdict while smiling: the plan is doomed and noble at once, romantic in its doomedness. Call a filing system impractical; save quixotic for doomed crusades you half admire.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    realistic: sizes up the odds and plans inside them

    hardheaded: unmoved by romance; runs on evidence and arithmetic

    Where it sits · how far dreams are carried into action

    hopeful → starry-eyed → quixotic

    hopeful vs. quixotic: Hopeful is the mild, grounded end — expecting things may go well — far short of quixotic, which mounts a campaign the arithmetic has already condemned.

    starry-eyed vs. quixotic: Starry-eyed dreamers ignore the odds, but they stop short of quixotic: the quixotic go one step past dreaming and actually ride at the windmill.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      affectionate / literary

      Quixotic smiles while it doubts: use it for noble long shots you half admire — a one-boat cleanup, a solo reforestation. 'Impractical' is the same verdict without the affection. Its unit-mate fatalistic is its mirror opposite: the fatalist has ruled that effort changes nothing, while the quixotic soul stakes everything on effort the numbers have already vetoed.
