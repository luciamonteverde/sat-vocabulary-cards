apathetic

/ap-uh-THET-ik/ - rhymes with athletic

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Apathetic means lacking interest, emotion, or concern.

See the word in context

The gym teacher could handle rowdy classes; what defeated him was an apathetic one — twenty students who would neither play nor protest.

The block association's founder papered every mailbox for the spring cleanup, offered free bagels, and moved the date twice for everyone's convenience. Four people came. The rest were not opposed and not busy — knock on any door and you met the same mild shrug. The neighborhood was not hostile to the cleanup; it was apathetic.

Apathetic is stronger than uninterested and different from lazy: lazy people dodge effort, and uninterested people simply are not curious, but apathetic names a missing feeling — there is no spark of concern to act on in the first place.

    Accuracy

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    listless: Listless is about missing energy rather than missing concern: a listless swimmer drags through laps she could normally fly through. Apathetic is about the concern itself — the feeling that should drive the effort never arrives. A listless person may care deeply and simply be drained; an apathetic one has nothing to summon.

    indifferent: Indifferent just reports no preference or concern about a particular thing, and it passes no judgment — a judge should be indifferent between the parties. Apathetic is the criticism: the capacity to care itself has drained away, across things that ought to matter, and the not-caring is the problem.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    passionate: full of strong feeling

    engaged: actively involved and invested

    Where it sits · how little feeling is left

    lukewarm → uninterested → apathetic → numb

    lukewarm vs. apathetic: A lukewarm response still holds a little warmth; apathetic means the warmth never arrives at all.

    uninterested vs. apathetic: Uninterested is narrow and mild — one topic fails to attract you; apathetic is wider and heavier, a lack of feeling about things that ought to matter.

    numb vs. apathetic: Numb goes even further than apathetic: the apathetic voter cannot be bothered to care, while a numb one has stopped feeling anything whatsoever, often from shock.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral / critical

      Apathetic is a diagnosis, and usually a criticism: the capacity to care has gone missing. Its cluster-mates carry different charges — indifferent just reports no preference or concern about a particular thing, and nonchalant describes a calm surface that may hide real feeling. Call someone apathetic only when the not-caring itself is the problem.
