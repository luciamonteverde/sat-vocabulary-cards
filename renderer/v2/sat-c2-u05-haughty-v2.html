haughty

/HAW-tee/ - rhymes with naughty

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Haughty means arrogantly superior and disdainful.

See the word in context

The soprano's haughty glance at the second violins said, more clearly than words, that she considered them furniture.

At the farmers market, the new cheese vendor waved away questions with a haughty little sigh, explaining that customers who had to ask the price 'were not really her customers.' Stall neighbors who offered friendly advice got the same look, delivered over the same raised chin.

Haughty is sharper than proud: pride can be quiet, private, even earned. Haughty is pride turned outward and downward - it needs someone to look down on. The chin lifts precisely so the eyes can descend.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    arrogant: Arrogant means claiming superiority - talking over people, assuming you deserve the first and last word - and it can be loud or entirely matter-of-fact. Haughty is one visible flavor of arrogance: cold, silent, and worn on the face, all lifted chin and withering glance.

    snobbish: Snobbish looks down through one narrow lens: class and taste - the right schools, the right shoes, the right accent. Haughty needs no checklist; it is a general air of icy superiority that can descend on anyone at all, whatever their standing or style.

    lofty: Lofty means elevated and grand - lofty peaks, lofty ideals - and only sometimes sneers; a lofty goal can be admirable. Haughty is never a compliment: the elevation is all in the chin, and its one purpose is to place other people below it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    humble: makes no claim to superiority

    unassuming: quietly declines attention or rank

    Where it sits · how far scorn for others goes

    proud → haughty → contemptuous

    proud vs. haughty: Proud can be quiet and deserved - pride in your work looks down on no one, while haughty always finds someone beneath it.

    contemptuous vs. haughty: Contemptuous goes further than haughty: the haughty diner looks down on the waiter, while the contemptuous one sneers at him out loud.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral / literary

      Haughty is vivid and a touch literary - the word for visible, chilly superiority: the lifted chin, the withering glance. Arrogant is the everyday workhorse; snobbish targets class and taste. Reach for haughty when you want readers to see the look on the face.
