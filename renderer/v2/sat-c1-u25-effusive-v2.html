effusive

/ih-FYOO-siv/

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Effusive means expressing feelings in an unrestrained, gushing, overflowing way.

See the word in context

Her effusive welcome — hugs, happy tears, and three toasts before dinner — left the quiet exchange student blinking.

When the intern's small bug fix went live, the founder did not simply say thanks. He called an all-hands meeting, compared her to the company's original architects, sent a bouquet to her desk, and posted three paragraphs of praise on the internal board — with exclamation points. The intern, who had expected a nod at most, hid behind her monitor. Colleagues agreed the praise was heartfelt but almost comically effusive.

A meeting, a bouquet, and three gushing paragraphs for one small fix show feeling overflowing far past any restraint — that unrestrained outpouring is what effusive means.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    gushing: Gushing means pouring out emotion in an open flood — thanks, praise, or delight spilling out faster than anyone asked for. Effusive names the same overflow as a whole manner: an effusive person's expression, gestures, and words all run past normal restraint, not just the torrent of talk.

    unrestrained: Unrestrained means without any holding back, and it can describe almost anything — laughter, spending, criticism, applause. Effusive is unrestrained feeling in particular, expressed in a gushing, overflowing way. A debate can be unrestrained; only an outpouring of emotion is effusive.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    reserved: keeping feelings held in

    understated: deliberately restrained in expression

    Where it sits · how far feeling spills past normal bounds

    warm → effusive → cloying

    warm vs. effusive: Warm is weaker than effusive: a warm greeting shows real friendliness within normal bounds, while an effusive one overflows them entirely.

    cloying vs. effusive: Cloying is stronger than effusive — cloying gush is so excessive it sickens the listener, while effusive display can still feel genuine, if overwhelming.
