Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Goad means to prod or taunt someone into doing something.

See the word in context

The parrot goaded the old cat mercilessly — a whistle, a pause, a tap on the cage bars — until the cat finally sprang and knocked over the lamp.

The talk-show host spent the whole segment goading his guest — misquoting her book, praising her rival — until she snapped and said exactly the unguarded thing his producers wanted.

Unlike urging or encouraging, goading works through irritation: jabs, taunts, and needling designed to sting a person into moving. The goader doesn't ask you to act — he makes staying still feel unbearable.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    prod: To prod is to nudge someone repeatedly toward action — a poke, a reminder, another poke — without the sting. Goading keeps the pushing but adds the barb: its jabs are designed to irritate a person into moving. A prod hurries you along; a goad provokes you into reacting.

    taunt: To taunt is to jeer — mockery that may not aim at any action at all; the insult itself is the point. Goading borrows the taunt's sting and gives it a destination: every jab is meant to push the target into doing something. A taunt wounds; a goad steers.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    soothe: to settle someone down instead of working them up

    deflate: to take the wind out of someone entirely

    Where it sits · how deliberately it drives someone to act

    tease → needle → goad

    tease vs. goad: Teasing is far milder than goading — playful poking that wants a laugh, not a reaction.

    needle vs. goad: Needling is a step short of goading — the jabs sting and repeat, but goading aims every jab at one target: making the person finally act.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    vivid / informal

    Goad is the word for provocation with a purpose — the needling that wants a reaction. Use 'tease' when it's all in fun and 'taunt' when the mockery is the whole point; goad means the jabs are steering you somewhere. Its unit-mates reach the same destination by kinder roads: exhort cheers you into acting, and galvanize shocks you into it.
