beseech

/bih-SEECH/ - rhymes with reach

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Beseech means to beg or plead desperately, with pride set aside and everything at stake.

See the word in context

The letter from the front does not ask so much as beseech: send word, send anything, tell me you are alive.

When the sentence was read, the defendant's mother rose from the second row to beseech the judge — hands pressed together, voice breaking — to let her son serve his term near home. The bailiff started forward; the judge waved him back and let her finish.

Beseech is the exact verb because it means to beg or plead desperately — this is asking with the pride stripped out of it, urgent enough to interrupt a courtroom. Urge is a firm push and request is paperwork; beseech is what remains when neither has worked and everything is at stake.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    implore: To implore is to plead with desperate urgency — a near twin of beseech, only a shade more bookish. Both belong to moments when everything is at stake and pride has stopped mattering; if the asking is calm or routine, neither word fits.

    entreat: To entreat is to ask earnestly and gently, almost humbly — the asker looks up at the person being asked, and an entreaty can still be declined politely. To beseech is that plea grown desperate, all pride set aside, the voice already breaking.

    petition: To petition is to ask an authority through proper form — a written request, signatures, the official channel. To beseech is raw and personal: one voice pleading with the pride stripped out. A petition can sit in an in-box for weeks; beseeching happens in the moment, out loud.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    command: tells instead of asks, with power behind it

    brush off: waves the plea away unheard

    Where it sits · how much dignity the asker surrenders

    urge → beseech → grovel

    urge vs. beseech: Urge sits well below beseech — to urge is to press with composure and move on, the way a doctor urges rest and heads to the next chart, while to beseech is to plead with the pride stripped out.

    grovel vs. beseech: Grovel goes further than beseech — the pleading collapses into self-abasement, forehead to the floor, dignity traded away entirely.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / dramatic

    Beseech is begging in its Sunday clothes — save it for pleas where dignity has stepped aside: mercy, rescue, a life's favor. For daily asking, ask or urge; this unit's clamor is many voices demanding at volume, while beseech is one voice at someone's feet.
