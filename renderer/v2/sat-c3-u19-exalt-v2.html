What It Means

Exalt means to glorify — to lift someone or something high in praise, above everything around it.

See the word in context

Sports radio spent the whole week exalting the rookie goalie, replaying her double save until the clip wore thin.

The seed catalog does not merely describe the Moonrise tomato; it exalts it — 'queen of the August garden, rival to none, the fruit by which all others are judged.'

Exalt means to lift your subject high above everything around it — praise with altitude. Praise says 'well done'; exalt builds a pedestal. And keep it clear of its near-twin in this unit: you exalt someone else, but you exult — no a — when the joy is your own.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    glorify: To glorify is to bathe a person or deed in glory — praise polished until it shines. To exalt is to lift the subject bodily above everything around it, onto the pedestal. Both stay on the human side of the line: a glorified general and an exalted one are still men, just brightly lit.

    idolize: To idolize is to worship someone the way a fan worships a star — adoration that lives in the admirer's own heart. To exalt is a public act: hymns, monuments, speeches that lift the subject high for all to see. Idolizing happens inside you; exalting is performed out loud.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    belittle: to talk something down to a smaller size

    degrade: to drag down in worth or dignity

    Where it sits · how high the pedestal rises

    praise → exalt → deify

    praise vs. exalt: Praise is the ground floor — sincere approval, no pedestal yet; exalting begins where plain praise leaves off.

    deify vs. exalt: Deify goes further than exalt — to exalt heroes is to raise them high; to deify them is to stop calling them human at all.

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      formal / literary

      Exalt is elevated language for elevated praise — hymns, odes, monuments, anthems. In ordinary talk, praise or celebrate carries the load. The trap to defuse is one letter deep: exalt lifts someone else up; exult (this unit) is your own joy leaping out. An anthem exalts a homeland; a crowd exults when the whistle blows.
