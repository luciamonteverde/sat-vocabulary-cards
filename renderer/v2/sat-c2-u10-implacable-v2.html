Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Implacable means impossible to appease or calm.

See the word in context

Ten years of birthday cards changed nothing; Aunt Marisol stayed implacable about the borrowed, never-returned pearls.

When the Hendersons' oak dropped a limb through his greenhouse, Mr. Voss refused the apology, refused the repair crew, and refused the replacement panes bought and paid for in advance. Fruit baskets came back unopened; a driveway-shoveling campaign one snowy winter earned a single nod and no thaw. Twenty years on, he still turns his back at the mailbox. He is implacable.

Angry passes; even furious burns itself out. Implacable is a different claim: the anger has no price. The word doesn't measure heat — it reports that every instrument of soothing has been tried on this person and has failed, and that the next attempt will fail too.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    unappeasable: Unappeasable makes the same claim in transparent parts — no offering will ever satisfy — and it fastens onto appetites and demands as readily as anger: an unappeasable hunger, an unappeasable crowd. Implacable is the darker, narrower word for a person whose settled hostility no apology or gift will soften.

    relentless: Relentless is about pressure that never lets up — the relentless questioning, the relentless pace — force applied and kept on. Implacable faces the other direction: it measures what comes in, reporting that no apology, plea, or peace offering will ever soften the anger. One never stops pushing; the other can never be soothed.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    forgiving: ready to pardon and move on

    merciful: letting someone off the worst of it

    Where it sits · how little anything softens the anger

    displeased → unforgiving → implacable

    displeased vs. implacable: Someone displeased is unhappy about it today and over it by the weekend; implacable anger is the opposite pole — years of apologies leave it exactly where it began.

    unforgiving vs. implacable: The unforgiving never pardon the offense; the implacable go further — no apology, gift, or offering will even lower the temperature.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / ominous

    Implacable says every instrument of soothing has failed or will: implacable enemies, implacable critics, an implacable sense of grievance. Its unit-mate irascible measures how fast anger lights; implacable measures whether it can ever be put out. The irascible flare and cool; the implacable never cool at all.
