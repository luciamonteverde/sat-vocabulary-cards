antithetical

/an-tih-THET-ih-kul/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Antithetical means directly opposed or contrasted; being the exact opposite of something.

See the word in context

Rushing the decision through in a single afternoon was antithetical to the committee's long tradition of slow, careful review.

A hiking club wrote one rule into its founding charter: every trip must be open to beginners. Years later, a faction proposed invitation-only expeditions limited to elite climbers. Longtime members objected that closed trips were antithetical to the club's founding rule, the exact opposite of the openness it promised. The proposal failed, because a club built on welcoming newcomers could not adopt a policy dedicated to shutting them out.

The invitation-only policy was the direct opposite of the club's founding principle of openness, which is exactly what antithetical means.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    contrary: Contrary means completely opposed in nature or direction, like a contrary wind that pushes against your course. Antithetical is the stronger, more formal claim: one thing is the exact mirror opposite of another, usually a principle or value. Contrary things push against each other; antithetical things cancel each other out.

    opposed: Opposed means set against something - a person, plan, or force in active conflict with another - and the opposition can be partial or negotiable. Antithetical says something stricter: the two things are exact opposites in principle, with no overlap possible. You can be mildly opposed to a plan; a plan is antithetical to a value it reverses.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    identical: exactly the same

    aligned: in agreement or matching in direction

    Where it sits · how deep the clash between them runs

    different → antithetical → irreconcilable

    different vs. antithetical: Two ideas can be different and still sit comfortably side by side, while antithetical ideas are direct opposites of each other.

    irreconcilable vs. antithetical: Antithetical positions are exact opposites, but irreconcilable ones have hardened so completely that no compromise between them will ever be possible.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal, academic and argumentative register

    Antithetical almost always appears with 'to' (antithetical to our values, antithetical to the scientific method) and names direct, principled opposition: one thing is the exact opposite of another. Save it for genuine opposites; for things that are merely different or mildly at odds, choose a weaker word.
