Meaning

What It Means

Compliant means meeting the rules and willing to follow them.

See the word in context

The dairy kept its labels compliant with the new dating law, reprinting every carton a month before the deadline.

A startup's first government contract came with a checklist: captions on every video, alt text on every image, forms a screen reader could navigate. The lead designer rebuilt the site until it was compliant with every line, ran the audit twice, and filed the report early — not because she loved the rules, but because the contract lived or died by them.

Compliant is the exact word for that site, because compliant means meeting the rules and willing to follow them — the checklist is satisfied, point by point. Agreeable is about manner, a pleasant way with people; compliant is about written codes, and no amount of charm passes the audit for you.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    dutiful: Dutiful people do what duty asks of them, with a note of moral seriousness — the dutiful nurse stays late because it is right. Compliant is cooler and more clerical: it means the written rules are met, and a form, a factory, or a filing can be compliant with no sense of duty at all.

    amenable: Amenable describes an attitude: open to suggestion, easily persuaded to go along — an amenable colleague says yes before the plan is even finished. Compliant is about the rulebook, not the mood: a site or a filing is compliant when every written requirement is met, whatever anyone feels.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    resistant: pushing back against what is asked

    rebellious: defying the rules to make a point

    Where it sits · how much of one's own will bends

    cooperative → compliant → submissive

    cooperative vs. compliant: Cooperative bends less of your own will than compliant does — two teammates trading suggestions are cooperating as equals, rulebook or none, while compliant means a written code is met point by point.

    submissive vs. compliant: Submissive goes further than compliant — compliant means following the written rules, while submissive means yielding your own will to another person's.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / procedural

    Compliant lives on checklists: sites, factories, and filings are compliant with codes. Its unit-mates split the territory — abide by is the personal act of accepting a decision, obedience answers a person's commands, docile describes a temperament that is easy to lead, and servile is eagerness to please gone past dignity. Compliant is the least personal of the family: the rule is written, and the box is checked.
