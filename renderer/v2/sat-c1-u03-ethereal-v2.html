ethereal

/ih-THEER-ee-uhl/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Ethereal means extremely delicate, light, and graceful in a way that seems too perfect or otherworldly for ordinary life.

See the word in context

The soprano's ethereal high notes seemed to float above the orchestra rather than come from an actual human voice.

Photographs of the valley at dawn show mist curling low over the fields while the first sunlight turns the fog gold, giving the whole scene an ethereal quality, as if the landscape belonged to a dream rather than to any real place.

Nothing in the photograph is literally magical, but the delicate mist and soft golden light make the scene feel impossibly light and otherworldly — exactly the quality "ethereal" names.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    otherworldly: Otherworldly says something seems not to belong to ordinary reality at all — eerie, alien, or divine. Ethereal is gentler and more specific: the unreal impression comes from delicacy and lightness. A ghost story is otherworldly; a soprano's floating high note is ethereal.

    delicate: Delicate describes fine, fragile lightness — lace, thin porcelain, a light touch. Ethereal starts there but adds the otherworldly: something so delicate and graceful it seems too perfect for ordinary life. Everything ethereal is delicate; very little that is delicate rises to ethereal.

    dreamy: Dreamy means pleasantly unreal, as if drifting through a dream — soft, hazy, relaxed. Ethereal shares the unreality but insists on delicacy and grace: a heavy fog can make a street feel dreamy, while ethereal light seems too fine and perfect for this world.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    earthy: grounded, solid, and rooted in ordinary physical life

    substantial: solid and weighty, the opposite of delicate lightness

    Where it sits · how close to otherworldly it feels

    light → airy → ethereal

    light vs. ethereal: Something light simply doesn't feel heavy — a light scarf — with none of the otherworldly quality that makes a thing ethereal.

    airy vs. ethereal: Something airy feels open and weightless, like a sunlit room, but stays an everyday pleasure; ethereal goes further, toward a delicacy that seems too perfect for ordinary life.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    literary, poetic

    Ethereal is a literary, somewhat elevated word. It's reserved for things whose lightness or delicacy feels almost magical — music, light, fabric, or atmosphere — and would sound out of place describing something ordinary or heavy.
