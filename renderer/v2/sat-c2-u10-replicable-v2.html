Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Replicable means able to be repeated by others with the same results.

See the word in context

The safety training proved replicable: any branch that followed its nine written steps saw the same drop in accidents.

For a century, one family guarded the violin varnish. Then a retired chemist spent four years pinning down the numbers — resin ratios, kettle temperatures, drying humidity — and published them all. The recipe turned out to be replicable: workshops on three continents now pull the same amber gloss off the brush, and the 'secret' survives only as marketing.

Replicable is the difference between a feat and a method. A feat happens once and gathers legends; a method can be handed to a stranger and turn out the same result on demand. The word doesn't promise the result is good — only that it repeats, which is why laboratories, kitchens, and coaching staffs prize it equally.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    repeatable: Repeatable says the same hands can do it again — the same lab, the same kitchen, the same result tomorrow. Replicable raises the bar to strangers: hand the written method to a team that has never seen it, and the result still comes back. Every replicable method is repeatable; the reverse can fail.

    reproducible: Reproducible is the report-checker's word: take the original data and analysis, rerun them, and the same numbers fall out. Replicable makes the bolder claim — a fresh team, fresh materials, only the written recipe, and still the same finding. Reproducible re-checks the arithmetic; replicable re-runs the world.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    one-off: happens once, by luck or circumstance

    unrepeatable: beyond doing again, for anyone

    Where it sits · how firmly the finding is established

    suggestive → replicable → settled

    suggestive vs. replicable: A suggestive result hints that something real is there — once, in one place; replicable is the rank above it, earned only when strangers rerun the recipe and the same answer comes back.

    settled vs. replicable: Settled outruns replicable: a replicable finding repeats whenever strangers rerun it, while a settled one has repeated so often it lives in the textbooks.

    academic / scientific

    Replicable is the working scientist's seal: hand a stranger the recipe and the result comes back again. It pairs with its unit-mates falsifiable — testable at all — and tenable — still defensible after the test. A replicable recipe is a method; an unrepeatable one is a story.
