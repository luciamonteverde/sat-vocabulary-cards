Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Encore means an extra performance given because the audience demands it.

See the word in context

Stamping feet and whistles brought the magician back out for an encore: one final trick with a borrowed pocket watch.

Nineteenth-century opera crowds did not wait for the end: if an aria delighted them, they roared until the singer performed it again on the spot, and a single night's encores could stretch the show past midnight.

An encore is defined by who asks for it: the audience. The scheduled numbers are done, the bows are finished, and the extra piece exists only because the clapping would not die down. A planned bonus track is generosity; an encore is a demand honored.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    reprise: A reprise is the return of material the show has already presented once — the same music or scene coming around again later in the evening. An encore is defined by demand rather than material: it may repeat a favorite or be brand-new, but it exists only because the audience keeps clapping.

    bonus track: A bonus track is the extra song a band plans in advance and gives freely — generosity built into the album. An encore is never on the plan: it happens only because the crowd demands more once the program is over. Promised beforehand, the extra is a bonus, not an encore.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    opener: the first number of the night, played before anyone has asked for anything

    finale: the planned grand ending — an encore comes after even that

    Where it sits · how far past the planned program

    finale → curtain call → encore

    finale vs. encore: A finale is the program's own planned ending — impressive, but promised in advance, so it stops short of an encore.

    curtain call vs. encore: A curtain call comes closer — the performers return to face the applause — but it stops short of an encore, where they perform again.

      Word Choice

      everyday / performance world

      Encore is the exact word for the audience-demanded extra at a live show, and by extension for any repeat of a triumph — 'a debut season that will be hard to give an encore.' Keep reprise for material repeated inside the program, curtain call for bows alone, and finale for the planned big ending.
