Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Daunting means intimidating — so difficult or overwhelming in prospect that it makes you doubt your courage before you even begin.

See the word in context

On her first morning at the new job, the training binder alone looked daunting — three hundred pages to master before Friday.

The bar exam felt daunting to Marcus: two full days of testing that covered everything from three years of law school.

The exam's sheer size and high stakes make Marcus doubt himself before he has answered a single question. That is exactly what daunting captures: the task itself intimidates you and drains your courage.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    intimidating: Intimidating means making you feel nervous or outmatched, and anything can do it — a fierce boss, a marble lobby, a test. Daunting is narrower: it describes the task or prospect itself, whose sheer size or difficulty drains your courage before you begin. People are intimidating; the climb ahead is daunting.

    formidable: Formidable describes something whose size, power, or skill inspires fear and respect — a formidable opponent, a formidable intellect. The word admires the thing itself. Daunting keeps the focus on you: a daunting task is one that shakes your nerve at the prospect, whatever anyone else thinks of it.

    discouraging: Discouraging things drain your hope along the way — poor results, slow progress, a string of rejections. Daunting works earlier: a daunting task intimidates you before you have started, when nothing has gone wrong yet. Discouragement comes from setbacks; daunting is how the starting line itself can look.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    manageable: able to be handled without much trouble

    inviting: appealing; making you want to begin

    Where it sits · how much the task shakes your confidence

    challenging → daunting → overwhelming

    challenging vs. daunting: A challenging task stretches your skills but still feels doable; a daunting one makes you hesitate before you even start.

    overwhelming vs. daunting: Overwhelming goes further than daunting — the task no longer just intimidates you, it feels bigger than anything you could possibly handle.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Neutral / everyday and academic

    Daunting describes the task, never the person — the task is daunting, while the person facing it is daunted. It pairs most naturally with words like task, challenge, and prospect: 'a daunting challenge,' 'a daunting prospect.'
