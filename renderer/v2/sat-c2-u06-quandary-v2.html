quandary

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Quandary means a perplexed, unresolvable state.

See the word in context

The judging invitation put Chef Luis in a quandary: accept it, and his own mole recipe was out of the running.

The tide table put the kayak guide in a quandary: paddle back against the rising wind with a tired group, or wait out the chop and miss the harbor gate before it locked for the night.

Quandary names the guide's state exactly: two real options, each carrying a cost she cannot wish away, and no rule that sorts them. A predicament needs only trouble; a quandary needs a choice that refuses to sort itself out - and she is in one until the wind or the clock decides for her.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    dilemma: A dilemma is the two-horned choice itself — the classic word for a fork with exactly two options, both of them painful. A quandary is the state that such a choice puts you in: stuck, puzzled, circling. You face a dilemma; you are in a quandary.

    predicament: A predicament is any tight spot — a stalled car, an overdrawn account — whether or not a choice is involved at all. A quandary is specifically a chooser's trap: real options sit in front of you, each one costing something, and no rule sorts them.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    certainty: knowing exactly what to do next

    resolution: the knot untied - a decision actually made

    Where it sits · how badly the choice traps you

    indecision → quandary → paralysis

    indecision vs. quandary: Indecision is the everyday wobble between menu items; a quandary goes further - a knot where every option costs something real.

    paralysis vs. quandary: Paralysis is the far end: in a quandary you still weigh your options, but in decision paralysis the weighing never ends and no choice ever comes.
