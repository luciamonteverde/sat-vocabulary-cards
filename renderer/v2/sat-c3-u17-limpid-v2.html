Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Limpid means clear, transparent.

See the word in context

The portrait's limpid green eyes seem lit from behind, and visitors swear they follow you.

Reviewers keep praising the translator's limpid English: sentences you look straight through to the meaning, with nothing clouded and nothing showing off.

Limpid is stronger than plain 'clear' because it suggests a beautiful, effortless transparency — the quality of spring water carried over to eyes, air, voices, or prose. Writing can be clear yet clumsy; limpid writing is clear the way still water is.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    crystalline: Crystalline means clear as cut crystal — a close twin, sharing the see-through purity. Limpid adds ease and beauty: the transparency feels effortless, like spring water, and it carries into eyes, voices, and prose. Crystalline stresses the hard, faceted brilliance; limpid stays soft and still.

    glassy: Glassy describes a surface smooth and still as glass — a claim about the top alone, saying nothing about what lies beneath. Limpid is the opposite claim: you see straight through, the whole depth clear. A pond can be glassy at dawn and still hide everything beneath the surface.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    murky: so clouded nothing shows through

    turbid: stirred thick with sediment

    Where it sits · how fully see-through it is

    clean → clear → limpid

    clean vs. limpid: Clean means free of dirt but says nothing about seeing through; it stops well short of limpid.

    clear vs. limpid: Clear goes further than clean, yet stops short of limpid, which is so transparent it hardly seems to be there.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    literary

    Limpid flatters whatever it touches — water, eyes, a singing tone, prose — by making clarity beautiful. Don't confuse it with limp (drooping); the two are unrelated. And keep it apart from this unit's manifest: limpid describes the medium you see through, manifest the fact that stands in plain view.
