probity

/PROH-bih-tee/ - the stress falls on PROH

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Probity means honesty and integrity proven over time.

See the word in context

The referee's forty-year reputation for probity ended every argument on the pitch: if she called it out, it was out.

When the estate sale turned up a second, later will, the family lawyer drove it to the rival heirs' attorney the same afternoon - though it cut his own client's share in half. Colleagues called it probity of the old school: honesty maintained even against interest, because the alternative was unthinkable to him.

Probity is more than honesty in the moment; it is tested, documented integrity - the kind that holds when no one is watching and when telling the truth costs you. You can be honest by accident; probity is a record.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    uprightness: Uprightness is firm, visible adherence to what is right — a moral posture others can watch you hold. Probity adds proof across time: not just standing straight today, but a documented record of honest dealing that auditors and courts can verify.

    rectitude: Rectitude is strict correctness of principle — doing the right thing because the rule demands it, every time. Probity cares less about the rule than about the record: integrity demonstrated over years of dealings, the kind a career of clean ledgers proves.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    crookedness: bent dealing, especially with money

    dishonesty: any straying from the truth

    Where it sits · how much proof stands behind it

    honesty → integrity → probity

    honesty vs. probity: Honesty is telling the truth when asked; probity is a whole career of it, provable in the ledgers.

    integrity vs. probity: Integrity is moral wholeness you claim for yourself; probity goes a step further - integrity so tested and documented that the auditors vouch for it.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      formal / literary

      Probity is the vocabulary of audits, courts, and obituaries - integrity with a paper trail. Say honesty for everyday truth-telling. Its neighbor in this unit, propriety, is about observing the forms; probity is about being sound underneath them. A banquet can run with perfect propriety under a treasurer with no probity at all.
