enthrall

/en-THRAWL/ - rhymes with crawl

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Enthrall means to hold spellbound; to charm completely.

See the word in context

The serialized mystery enthralled the whole town; the librarian kept a waiting list forty names long for each new installment.

The new aquarium's octopus enthralls visitors so completely that the staff posts closing-time warnings at its tank alone: people miss ferries watching it unscrew jar lids and pour itself through a gap the width of a coin.

Enthrall began as a word for slavery: a thrall was a captive, and to enthrall was to enslave. The chains have gone figurative — what enthralls you today holds your attention captive, so pleasantly that you forget to check the time.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    mesmerize: To mesmerize is to hold someone as if hypnotized — a trance-still, stare-fixed grip, named for the hypnotist Mesmer. Enthrall shares the captive attention but adds delight and animation: an enthralled audience laughs and leans in, while a mesmerized one sits frozen, eyes locked.

    rivet: To rivet is to lock attention in place, pleasant or not — a horror film or a car crash can rivet you. Enthrall implies the grip is delightful: what enthralls holds you happily captive, so you forget the time rather than wish you could look away.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    bore: to let the attention slip loose and wander off

    weary: to wear interest down until nothing holds it

    Where it sits · how tightly it grips the attention

    interest → enthrall → obsess

    interest vs. enthrall: Interest opens the door — an interested reader keeps going, but nothing yet grips her; it stops well short of enthrall.

    obsess vs. enthrall: Obsess goes further than enthrall — an enthralled reader forgets the time; an obsessed one forgets everything else.

      Word Choice

      vivid / storytelling

      Enthrall for delight that holds attention captive — audiences, readers, children at a tide pool. Mesmerize when the holding feels hypnotic, rivet when it may not be pleasant at all. Its unit-mate enamor points at the heart instead: an enthralled visitor cannot look away; an enamored one comes back tomorrow in love.
