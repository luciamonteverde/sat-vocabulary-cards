vivacious

/vih-VAY-shuss/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Vivacious means full of lively, charming energy and spirit, especially in social settings.

See the word in context

Everyone's vivacious great-aunt Rosa could turn even a rainy reunion into a party.

The new intern, Bea, learned every name on the floor by Wednesday. She spun her chair around to join conversations, organized a Friday trivia lunch, and somehow made the quarterly budget meeting feel like a game show. "We hired a spreadsheet analyst," her manager laughed, "and got a vivacious master of ceremonies."

Bea's sparkling, people-focused energy — learning names, launching trivia, making meetings lively — is exactly the charming social liveliness that vivacious describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    lively: Lively means full of energy — a lively debate, a lively puppy, a lively market can all bustle without charming anyone. Vivacious is more particular: the energy is social and winning, sparkle aimed at the people in the room. Lively measures motion; vivacious measures charm.

    spirited: Spirited means energetic and bold — a spirited defense, a spirited reply, energy with some fight in it. Vivacious keeps the energy but trades the fight for charm: warm, social sparkle that draws people in rather than squaring off with them.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    dull: lacking energy or interest

    sluggish: slow-moving and lifeless

    Where it sits · how much social energy pours out

    cheerful → vivacious → irrepressible

    cheerful vs. vivacious: Cheerful describes a pleasant, sunny mood; vivacious adds sparkle — an energy that actively charms and animates the people nearby.

    irrepressible vs. vivacious: Irrepressible is stronger than vivacious: liveliness so overflowing that nothing and no one can dampen it.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    warmly admiring; almost always describes a person

    Vivacious praises one person's charming, engaging social sparkle, while its louder cousin boisterous (also in this unit) describes noisy, rowdy exuberance — a whole crowd can be boisterous, but only a sparkling personality is vivacious.
