Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Environmentalism means the movement dedicated to protecting the natural world.

See the word in context

Her environmentalism lives in small habits — the bike commute, the rain barrels, the patient letters to the water board.

For the retired trawler captain, environmentalism meant one unglamorous chore: hauling other boats' torn nets out of the bay, every Sunday, for nineteen years.

Environmentalism is the -ism of the environment: not a single cleanup or a recycling habit, but the organized movement — and the conviction behind it — that the natural world needs defending in law, budget, and habit. One person can practice it; the word itself names the cause.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    ecology: Ecology is the science of how living things interact with each other and their surroundings — coursework, field data, peer review. Environmentalism is not a science but a cause: the organized movement pressing laws, budgets, and habits into the natural world's defense. One is studied; the other is fought for.

    stewardship: Stewardship is the quiet ethic of taking care of what is in your charge — a farm kept healthy, a trust kept whole — answerable mostly to your own conscience. Environmentalism is public and organized: a movement that recruits, campaigns, and legislates far beyond any one person's plot.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    exploitation: treating the natural world purely as a resource to use up

    wastefulness: the everyday habit the movement organizes against

    Where it sits · how broad and active the commitment

    appreciation → conservation → environmentalism

    appreciation vs. environmentalism: Appreciation asks nothing of you — loving the view from the trail stops far short of environmentalism.

    conservation vs. environmentalism: Conservation goes further than appreciation but stops short of environmentalism: it stewards particular places and stocks, while the -ism presses to change laws, budgets, and habits everywhere.
