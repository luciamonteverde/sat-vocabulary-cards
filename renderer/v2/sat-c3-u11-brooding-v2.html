brooding

/BROO-ding/ - the stress falls on BROO

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Brooding means sunk in dark, silent thought — dwelling on a trouble, turning it over and over.

See the word in context

All autumn the benched quarterback grew brooding, replaying the interception on his phone long after the film sessions ended.

The novelist gave her detective a brooding streak: between cases he sat for hours over the one file he had never closed, turning its dead ends over in his mind while the tea went cold beside him.

That is the word's whole anatomy: a dark thought, held in silence, kept warm for a long time. The image underneath is the hen on her nest — to brood is literally to sit on eggs until they hatch — and a brooding person sits on a trouble the same way. The word also stretches to things that look heavy with something about to break: a brooding sky, a brooding castle on the cliff.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    sullen: Sullen is gloom worn on the outside — the scowl, the short answers, the refusal to join in. Brooding turns the gloom inward: the dark mood has a thought at the center of it, being turned over and over.

    moody: Moody means given to shifting spells of gloom that come and go without much cause. Brooding is one dark spell with an object — a brooding person is stuck on something in particular.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    carefree: untroubled, with nothing gnawing underneath

    breezy: light and untroubled in manner

    Where it sits · how dark the dwelling on a thought turns

    pensive → brooding → tormented

    pensive vs. brooding: Pensive is milder than brooding — absorbed in thought, but the thinking can be calm, even pleasant; brooding shuts the door and turns the thought dark.

    tormented vs. brooding: Tormented is stronger than brooding: the dark thought stops being dwelt on and starts doing the hurting, giving its owner no rest at all.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    dark / literary

    Brooding is the reviewer's word for dark interiority — a brooding hero, a brooding score, a brooding stretch of coastline. Its unit-mate morose names settled ill-tempered gloom with no particular object; brooding always has something at its center, a trouble being sat on. Applied to places and skies, it means they look like they're doing the same thing.
