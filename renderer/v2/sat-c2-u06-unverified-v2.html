What It Means

Unverified means not yet checked or confirmed as true.

See the word in context

The class wiki shades every unverified figure yellow until an editor traces it to a source.

Family legend says Great-Grandpa pitched an inning against a touring big-league team in 1934. The story is unverified - no scorecard, no clipping, no roster - which is not the same as false; it waits, patiently, for somebody to check the county archive.

Unverified is precise because it passes no judgment either way: the story may be perfectly true - it simply has not been checked against anything. The moment someone finds the 1934 scorecard, it graduates to verified; if the archive shows the team never toured, it falls apart. Until then it waits.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    unconfirmed: Unconfirmed means no authority has yet said yes: the announcement, the official word, the sign-off has not come. Unverified points at the checking itself — nobody has yet tested the claim against records, witnesses, or sources. A report can be unconfirmed simply because the confirming call never happened.

    untested: Untested describes something never yet put through a trial — a rookie goalie, a new engine design, a theory awaiting its first experiment. Unverified is narrower and belongs to claims: a statement nobody has yet checked against records or sources. Things go untested; assertions go unverified.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    proven: shown true by test or evidence

    authenticated: certified genuine by an expert check

    Where it sits · how far testing has turned against it

    unverified → unsubstantiated → discredited

    unsubstantiated vs. unverified: Unsubstantiated goes further than unverified: an unverified claim simply hasn't been checked yet, while an unsubstantiated one has been shopped around and no support has turned up.

    discredited vs. unverified: Discredited is the far end: the unverified claim awaits its test, the unsubstantiated claim lacks support, and the discredited claim took the test and failed it.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / journalistic

    Unverified takes no side at all: the checking has not happened, so belief should wait. The moment doubt sets in, the word is dubious (this unit); if support was sought and none found, unsubstantiated. And it applies to claims, not plans - a changeable schedule is tentative, not unverified.
