fragmentary

/FRAG-muhn-ter-ee/ - the stress falls on FRAG

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Fragmentary means made up of small disconnected pieces.

See the word in context

His memory of the crash is fragmentary - a horn, a blue fender, the smell of rain - with nothing joining the pieces.

Of Great-Aunt Rosa's bakery years the family holds only fragmentary traces: a flour-dusted apron, half a recipe card that ends at 'fold in the -', and one photograph missing its left third.

Fragmentary is right because the record survives only as disconnected scraps: an apron, half a card, a torn photo. Each piece is genuine - no one questions them - but no piece connects to the next, and most of the story lives in the gaps between.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    piecemeal: Piecemeal describes how something arrives or gets built — one scrap at a time, over stretches of time, with no overall plan. Fragmentary judges what remains after the losses are counted: most of the whole is missing, and scraps are all that survive.

    disjointed: A disjointed account has all its parts on hand, but they fail to connect or flow — the telling lurches from scene to scene. Fragmentary faults survival instead: most of the whole is simply gone, and what is left cannot connect because it barely exists.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    whole: all of it present in one piece

    unbroken: continuous from start to finish

    Where it sits · how little of the whole survives

    incomplete → patchy → fragmentary

    incomplete vs. fragmentary: An incomplete file is missing a page or two; fragmentary goes further down the scale of ruin - what survives is only scraps.

    patchy vs. fragmentary: Patchy coverage has holes you can map; fragmentary is worse still: no stretch is whole, and the pieces barely touch.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / scholarly

    Fragmentary judges completeness, nothing else: the surviving record is scraps. It pairs naturally with archives, memories, and fossils. Set it against circumstantial, this unit's evidence word - circumstantial evidence is indirect but can be plentiful and whole; fragmentary evidence is direct but survives only in pieces.
