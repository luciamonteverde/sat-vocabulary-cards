Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Relegate means to assign to a lower or less important position.

See the word in context

After one shaky rehearsal, the first-chair violinist found herself relegated to the back of the section.

Cousin Marco is forty-one, a father of two, and every Thanksgiving his aunts relegate him to the folding table with the toddlers and the juice boxes. The seat assignment comes with a clean bib and no explanation. He has argued his case for a decade; the aunts reply that the grown-ups' table is full, and somehow it always is.

Relegate is the exact word for what they do to him, because it means to assign to a lower or less important position — and everyone involved reads the message in the assignment. Demote covers rank at work; relegate covers people, columns, vases, and whole teams, and always carries the little sting of judgment.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    demote: To demote is to drop someone a rank at work — the plain paperwork word for a fall in job title. Relegate reaches far wider: people, dishes, columns, and whole teams can all be filed downward, and the new assignment always carries a sting of judgment.

    sideline: To sideline someone is to push them out of the action while keeping them on hand — benched, but still in uniform. Relegate goes a step further: it assigns a definite lower place, a worse address everyone can see, and the judgment travels with it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    elevate: raise to a higher place or rank

    showcase: put front and center for everyone to see

    Where it sits · how far the person's standing falls

    overlook → relegate → banish

    overlook vs. relegate: Overlook is milder than relegate — nobody assigns you anywhere when you are overlooked; you are simply not chosen this time, while relegate hands you a lower place on purpose.

    banish vs. relegate: Banish goes further than relegate — a relegated player still suits up, lower on the roster, while a banished one is off the team and out the door entirely.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / pointed

    Relegate always travels with a destination: relegated to the bench, to a footnote, to the second division. It means assigned downward, and everyone involved feels the judgment in it. Demote is the plain workplace word for rank; relegate covers people and things alike and keeps a chill of judgment. Inside this unit, rejuvenate lifts its subject up and away from decline; relegate files its subject down the order — opposite motions on the same staircase.
