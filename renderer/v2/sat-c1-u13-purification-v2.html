purification

/pyoor-uh-fih-KAY-shun/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Purification means the whole process of removing impurities, contaminants, or unwanted elements to make something clean or pure.

See the word in context

The town's new treatment plant handles the purification of river water, filtering out mud and bacteria before the water reaches anyone's tap.

Aboard the submarine, scrubbers run day and night for the purification of the crew's air, pulling carbon dioxide out of the cabin so that only breathable air keeps circulating.

The scrubbers do one thing: they remove an unwanted element, the carbon dioxide, so that what remains is clean and safe. Removing what does not belong until something is pure is the exact meaning of purification.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    cleansing: Cleansing is thorough washing or cleaning, usually of a surface or the body — a deep cleansing of skin or a wound. Purification goes all the way through: the whole process of removing impurities mixed into a substance until what remains is pure.

    decontamination: Decontamination is the removal of dangerous substances — radiation, toxins, hazardous chemicals — to make something safe. Safety is its whole aim. Purification aims at pureness itself: removing every unwanted element, harmful or not, until only the pure substance remains.

    filtration: Filtration means straining unwanted particles out of a liquid or gas by passing it through a barrier that traps them. It is one method among several. Purification is the whole process of removing impurities, whatever the method — filtering, treating, boiling, or all of them in stages.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    contamination: making something impure

    pollution: dirtying with harmful substances

    Where it sits · how thoroughly the unwanted is stripped away

    cleaning → purification → sterilization

    cleaning vs. purification: Cleaning removes visible dirt from a surface, while purification removes even the invisible impurities mixed all the way through something.

    sterilization vs. purification: Purification removes impurities until something is pure, while sterilization goes further and destroys every last microorganism.

      Formal / technical

      Most common in technical phrases like water purification and air purification. It is broader than filtration, which is just one method; purification names the whole process of removing impurities, whatever the method.

      The chip plant's most guarded building has nothing to do with circuits. It houses the purification line for silicon. Beach sand is already mostly silicon, an engineer explained, but mostly is the enemy: a few stray atoms of boron in a wafer can ruin the chip built on it. So the silicon is melted, drawn, treated with gases, and recrystallized -- not once but in repeated stages, each pass leaving fewer intruders behind. No single step does the job. The furnaces, the gas baths, and the slow pulling of the crystal all remove different unwanted elements. What emerges is called nine-nines silicon: 99.9999999 percent pure, clean all the way through, ready at last to become a wafer.
