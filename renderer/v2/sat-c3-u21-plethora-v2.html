What It Means

Plethora means an abundance, excess.

See the word in context

Grandma's junk drawer holds a plethora of keys — decades of them — though no one can name a single lock they open.

The new hires' welcome packet contained a plethora of passwords: badge codes, parking codes, seven single-use logins, and a printed warning not to write any of them down.

Plethora began as a doctor's diagnosis — an excess of blood — and the word still carries a symptom's edge: not just many, but more than is useful, an abundance that starts to get in the way.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    oversupply: Oversupply is the economist's word for a market where supply has outrun demand — warehouses filling because buyers stopped buying. A plethora needs no market at all: it is the livelier, everyday word for a pile of too many, from passwords to remote controls.

    deluge: A deluge is abundance arriving as a flood — applications, emails, complaints pouring in faster than anyone can respond, the emphasis all on the rush of arrival. A plethora is what remains once everything has landed: the standing pile of more than anyone can use.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    shortfall: less than what was counted on

    lack: an absence where something is needed

    Where it sits · how far supply outruns the need

    plenty → plethora → glut

    plenty vs. plethora: Plenty stops short of plethora — plenty is comfortably enough, while a plethora is already more than anyone asked for.

    glut vs. plethora: Glut goes further than plethora — a plethora crowds the shelf, but a glut breaks the system, burying prices, patience, or both.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / wry

    A plethora is rarely a pure compliment: the word counts past 'plenty' and sighs. For welcome abundance this unit gives you plenitude; for the harmful extreme, surfeit or glut. Reach for plethora when the pile itself is the point — options, emails, remote controls — more than anyone can use.
