Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Perfunctory means done as a routine duty with minimal effort and no real interest or enthusiasm — carried out just to get it over with.

See the word in context

The guard gave the badges a perfunctory glance, waving through anyone who held up something laminated.

Asked at the press conference about the outgoing minister, the premier offered a perfunctory tribute — two sentences of boilerplate thanks, read off a card — before turning eagerly to the next question.

The duty was technically met: a tribute was delivered. But the flat routine of it — no interest, no enthusiasm, no glance up from the card — is exactly what perfunctory names. The eager turn to the next question proves the energy existed; it just wasn't spent here.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    cursory: Cursory means hasty and skipping the details, usually because time is short; perfunctory adds indifference — there was time enough, but no interest in using it.

    halfhearted: Halfhearted means done with little energy because the doer's heart isn't in it; halfhearted still admits a trace of trying, while perfunctory is pure box-checking — form observed, effort withheld.

    automatic: Automatic means happening without conscious thought, like a reflex; perfunctory acts are chosen — the person could engage with the task, and doesn't.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    thorough: done with full care, nothing skipped

    heartfelt: done with genuine feeling

    Where it sits · how empty of effort the performance is

    cursory → perfunctory → token

    cursory vs. perfunctory: Cursory is milder than perfunctory: a cursory reading at least races through the whole text with intent, while a perfunctory one has stopped caring whether anything registers.

    token vs. perfunctory: Token goes beyond perfunctory: a perfunctory inspection is still an inspection, done without interest, but a token one exists only so the paperwork can say it happened.

      Word Choice

      cool / judging

      Perfunctory is an observer's verdict on effort, not on results — a perfunctory inspection can pass a safe bridge and still deserve the adjective. It attaches best to small performed rituals: nods, applause, apologies, glances. When speed is the point and interest is beside it, reach for cursory instead.

      For nineteen years Mr. Okafor's hotel inspections had been feared; lately they had become perfunctory. He still arrived precisely at nine, still carried the famous clipboard, and still visited every floor in the old order. But managers noticed that he no longer ran a finger along the doorframes, and that the boxes on his form were ticked in smooth unbroken runs, sometimes before a door was opened. He was not rushed — the tours took as long as ever — and he was not unkind. The routine simply continued without him inside it, and the hotels, one by one, quietly stopped being afraid.
