Meaning

What It Means

Malleable means capable of being shaped or transformed.

See the word in context

Pollsters found opinion on the new tax still malleable: one strong editorial moved the numbers eight points in a week.

Cognitive researchers ran the same interview three times, months apart, and watched the details drift: the getaway car changed color, a bystander gained an umbrella, and by the third telling the witness owned a version half her own making. Memory, they concluded, is malleable — each retelling presses it into a slightly new shape, and the new shape holds.

Malleable is the exact word because the shaping comes from outside and the result keeps: pressed once, the memory stays pressed. Its unit-mate pliable is the springier idea — bending without breaking, and often bending back. What is pliable yields; what is malleable is remade.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    moldable: Moldable is the hands-on, workshop twin: clay, dough, or warm plastic that fingers and tools can press into shape. It stays close to physical stuff you can touch. Malleable reaches further — metals, but also opinions, habits, and memories — anything outside pressure can remake into a form that then holds.

    impressionable: Impressionable is for minds, especially young ones: a person so easily influenced that whatever they meet leaves its mark. It rarely describes materials at all. Malleable covers both worlds — a malleable metal or a malleable opinion — and stresses that the pressure comes from outside and the new shape keeps.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    unyielding: refusing to give under any pressure

    rigid: stiff and fixed, impossible to shape

    Where it sits · how easily its form gives way

    workable → malleable → fluid

    workable vs. malleable: Workable clay takes a thumbprint with some effort; malleable promises more — the whole form can be remade under steady pressure.

    fluid vs. malleable: Fluid outruns malleable: a malleable thing takes a new shape and keeps it, while a fluid one holds no shape of its own at all.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / figurative-friendly

    Reach for malleable when outside pressure can reshape something and the new shape will hold — metals, opinions, young habits. Its unit-mate pliable stresses easy bending and bouncing back instead; a pliable person sways with the room, a malleable one is remade by it. For a scene that changes on its own energy, the word is dynamic, not malleable.
