What It Means

Curt means abruptly and rudely short.

See the word in context

Her curt 'Fine.' closed the phone call before her brother could get to his apology.

The new intern asked where the supply closet was and got a curt 'Second floor' tossed over the office manager's shoulder; by lunch she had learned to save her questions for anybody else.

Curt is shortness with an edge: brief means economical, but a curt reply is so short it insults. It usually lands on a single response — a curt nod, a curt 'No' — where its neighbor brusque colors someone's whole manner.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    clipped: Clipped means trimmed short in sound or phrasing — clipped vowels, a clipped telegram style — and it carries no rudeness by itself. Curt is short with an edge: a curt reply is so abrupt it insults, where clipped speech may only be efficient.

    snappish: Snappish is shortness with temper in it — quick, irritable answers that bite at whoever asked. Curt can wound without any anger at all: a curt 'No' is rude for its bare abruptness, while a snappish one snaps because the speaker is cross.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    gracious: generous and courteous in manner

    chatty: happily full of words

    Where it sits · how much scorn the shortness carries

    brief → curt → contemptuous

    brief vs. curt: Brief is milder than curt — a brief answer is just economical, with no rudeness in it.

    contemptuous vs. curt: Contemptuous is harsher than curt: a curt reply cuts you off, while a contemptuous one tells you the speaker thinks you were never worth answering.
