Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Heterogeneity means the state of being made up of parts that differ in kind — variety running all the way through one group or mixture.

See the word in context

Geologists prize the cliff face for its heterogeneity: sandstone, shale, and volcanic ash stacked in bands, each laid down by a different age.

Reviewers expected the anthology to read like one voice; instead its heterogeneity became the selling point — a ghost story beside a court transcript, a prose poem beside a recipe, twenty writers agreeing on nothing but the page count.

Heterogeneity is not mere assortment: it says the differences reach all the way down to kind. The anthology's pieces do not simply vary in topic — they belong to different species of writing altogether — and the word reports that all-the-way-down difference precisely, without praising or blaming it.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    diversity: Diversity is variety among the people or things in a group, and usually a word of welcome — a diverse newsroom, a diverse diet. Heterogeneity measures the same mixedness coolly, without applause, and insists the differences reach down to kind.

    variability: Variability is how much something changes across measurements or over time - one river's depth through the seasons. Heterogeneity is difference across the parts of one group at one time: not a value moving, but a mixture of unlike members.

    dissimilarity: Dissimilarity is the plain fact that two things are unlike each other. Heterogeneity scales that unlikeness up to a whole collection: one group whose many parts differ among themselves.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    homogeneity: sameness of kind running all the way through

    sameness: lack of variety; each part just like the next

    Where it sits · how deep the differences inside one whole run

    variety → heterogeneity → incongruity

    variety vs. heterogeneity: Variety is milder than heterogeneity: variety promises a pleasing surface assortment — several flavors, a few styles — while heterogeneity says the parts of the group differ more deeply, down to their very kinds.

    incongruity vs. heterogeneity: Incongruity goes further than heterogeneity: a group can show heterogeneity and still work as one whole, but incongruity means the differences have sharpened into a clash, and the pieces no longer seem to belong together.

      Word Choice

      academic / technical

      Heterogeneity belongs to lab reports, census tables, and policy reviews: samples, populations, and portfolios have heterogeneity. Everyday speech reaches for variety or diversity instead. Choose it when you are measuring a mixture rather than celebrating one - and keep its mirror straight: its clean opposite, "uniformity," is taught in this same unit.

What arrives instead is a torrent of unlike things - laptop chargers, garden hoses, ceramic mugs, bowling balls. Engineers call the problem the heterogeneity of the waste stream, and it defeats automation more reliably than volume ever has. A ballistic separator can shake paper from plastic, but no machine yet built expects a bowling ball; the parts of the stream differ not in amount but in kind. The plant's fix is human: forty sorters who can recognize anything. Machines handle sameness, the manager says. People handle the rest.
