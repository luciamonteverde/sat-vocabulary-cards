Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Voluminous means very large in volume or quantity.

See the word in context

The circus tent is voluminous — three rings, a band pit, and seating for a thousand under one sweep of striped cloth.

Aunt Marisol's handbag is voluminous in a way physics ought to question: it has yielded, on request, an umbrella, a sewing kit, two paperbacks, a thermos, and once — at a picnic short of dessert — an entire pineapple. The bag swallows a full arm to the elbow and gives no sign of filling.

Voluminous is the bulk word: great volume, whether swallowed (a bag, a tent) or supplied (files, letters). The root is Latin volumen, a scroll — writing rolled round and round until it filled the hand. Anything voluminous fills like that: in rolls, folds, and armfuls.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    bulky: Bulky means big and awkward in the carrying — one clumsy object that fights the arms and jams the doorway, with no grace about it. Voluminous is abundance of volume, in folds or in files: cloth by the armful, letters by the shelf. A bulky crate; a voluminous cloak.

    billowing: Billowing means swelling outward in soft folds as air moves through — sails filling, smoke rolling, curtains lifting at an open window. It is volume in motion. Voluminous is the volume itself, with everything still: a cloak folded flat in a trunk is not billowing, but it is every bit as voluminous.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    compact: packed small and neat

    skimpy: cut too small to cover the need

    Where it sits · how much slack the fit leaves

    snug → roomy → voluminous

    snug vs. voluminous: A snug jacket touches at every seam; voluminous is its opposite pole, cloth to spare in every direction.

    roomy vs. voluminous: Roomy leaves comfortable space inside; voluminous goes further — folds on folds, fabric or files by the armful, volume past all mere comfort.

      Word Choice

      formal / literary

      Voluminous measures bulk: garments that billow, files that fill shelves, tents that swallow crowds. Don't let the stereo mislead you — loud is about sound level, and a voluminous voice would be a big one only in a poet's joke. Among its unit-mates, copious counts the pouring quantity, replete describes the well-stocked container, and voluminous weighs the sheer roll and fold of the thing itself.
