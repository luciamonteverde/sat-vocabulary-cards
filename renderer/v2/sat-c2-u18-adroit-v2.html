What It Means

Adroit means skillful and dexterous.

See the word in context

The sitter proved adroit with the twins, trading the scissors they'd found for bubble wands before either could protest.

Mid-interview, the author was handed a question designed to start a feud with her rival. Her adroit reply — praising the rival's first book, quoting her own bad review of it, and laughing hardest at herself — left the host with no quarrel to print.

Adroit is the precise word because adroit means skillful and dexterous — quick, clever handling, whether of objects or of moments like this one. Tactful would only say she avoided offense; adroit credits the nimble skill of the move, a tricky thing caught mid-air and set down gently.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    slick: Slick is smooth skill with showmanship in it — the move announces its own polish, and the word often carries a raised eyebrow. Adroit is clean of that suspicion: clever, graceful handling praised without irony. A slick answer makes you check your wallet; an adroit one makes you admire the save.

    resourceful: Resourceful praises making do — clever use of whatever happens to be at hand, with nothing said about grace. Adroit praises the handling itself: a tricky object or moment managed smoothly and well. A resourceful camper improvises a tent pole; an adroit host settles the argument before anyone notices a rescue.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    clumsy: hands and moves that betray you

    ham-fisted: force where finesse was needed

    Where it sits · how much judgment guides the movement

    competent → nimble → adroit

    competent vs. adroit: Competent is milder than adroit — competent handling gets the tray to the table without applause or incident; adroit adds grace and judgment the moment the tray tips.

    nimble vs. adroit: Nimble is quick and light on its feet, but it is only the speed; adroit adds the judgment — the right move, made smoothly, at the tricky moment.

      Word Choice

      polished / appreciative

      Adroit praises clever, graceful handling — of tongs, of hecklers, of delicate moments. Beside its unit-mates: deft stays closest to the fingers, agile is quickness of body or mind, adept is broad mastery of a whole activity. Adroit is the word for the tricky moment handled beautifully.
