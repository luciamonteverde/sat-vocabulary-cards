Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Customized means altered from the standard version to fit one particular user or purpose.

See the word in context

The clinic's customized hearing aids are molded from an impression of each patient's ear, so no two on the shelf match.

After his grandmother's stroke left her cooking one-handed, Tomás customized her kitchen a drawer at a time. The cutting board grew steel pegs to hold vegetables still; the kettle sat in a cradle that tipped at a touch; her favorite pans moved to a shelf exactly at elbow height. Catalog gadgets had failed her for a year — every one designed for some average cook who wasn't her.

Customized is the right word because each change was fitted to one particular user: not a better kitchen in general, but her kitchen, measured against her reach and her working hand. That's the test the word sets — start from the standard thing, then bend it to the individual. The catalog's one-size gadgets are the perfect foil: generic is exactly what customized exists to correct.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    tailored: Tailored keeps the image of the fitting room — cutting and trimming what already exists to one wearer's measurements. Customized covers any product bent to its user, from software to saddles, with no cloth in sight.

    personalized: Personalized can be as light as a monogram — your name stamped on the standard thing. Customized goes deeper: the thing itself is altered until it fits one user's needs, not just labeled as theirs.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    generic: one version, made the same for everyone

    standardized: held identical to the common pattern

    Where it sits · how far the thing bends to one user

    adjusted → customized → bespoke

    adjusted vs. customized: Adjusted is milder than customized: an adjusted chair got one lever moved for you, while a customized one has been genuinely reworked around a single sitter.

    bespoke vs. customized: Bespoke goes further than customized — a customized product starts standard and is altered to fit, but a bespoke one is made to order from the first sketch and never existed in a standard version.
