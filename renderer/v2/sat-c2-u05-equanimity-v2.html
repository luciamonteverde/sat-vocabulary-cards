Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Equanimity means an even, steady calmness of temperament that holds up under stress.

See the word in context

Yoga teachers like to say the poses are only practice for traffic jams: the real prize is equanimity you can take off the mat.

When the printer mangled the first three hundred programs an hour before graduation, the events director sent one volunteer for scissors, another for card stock, and began hand-lettering replacements herself, chatting about the weather as she worked. Her equanimity was so complete that half the volunteers never realized anything had gone wrong.

Equanimity is more than calm, because calm can simply mean nothing is happening. Equanimity is evenness of mind maintained while things go wrong - pressure present, composure unbroken. The director isn't relaxed because the stakes are low; she is level because that is her setting.

    Accuracy

    “Does this word fit the meaning exactly, or does a neighbour fit better?”

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    poise: Poise is graceful assurance in how you carry yourself - the level voice, the unhurried walk, the ease of a practiced host. It is about bearing. Equanimity is about the mind behind the bearing: an even temperament that holds steady under real stress, whether or not anyone is watching.

    levelheadedness: Levelheadedness is sensible steadiness in judgment - the levelheaded person weighs the options and decides well in a crisis. It praises thinking. Equanimity praises temperament: the even, unshaken mood itself, maintained while things go wrong, quite apart from whatever decisions get made.

    serenity: Serenity is deep, untroubled peace - the stillness of a quiet lake or a mind at rest, usually when nothing is pressing on it. Equanimity earns its calm the hard way: evenness kept while things go wrong, with the pressure fully present and the steadiness unbroken.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    agitation: visible, restless upset

    panic: overwhelming fear that takes over

    Where it sits · how long the poise lasts under pressure

    calm → composure → equanimity

    calm vs. equanimity: Calm can simply mean nothing is stirring - a calm lake, a calm Sunday; equanimity is calm that holds while everything stirs.

    composure vs. equanimity: Composure is self-command in one difficult moment; equanimity goes further - an evenness so settled it holds across the whole storm, not just the scene.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / literary

    Equanimity is the elevated word for an even temper under pressure; it praises a durable trait, not one good moment. In everyday speech, say someone 'kept their cool' or 'stayed composed'; write equanimity when the steadiness itself is the person's signature.
