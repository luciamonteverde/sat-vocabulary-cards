refurbish

/ree-FUR-bish/ - the stress falls on FUR

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Refurbish means to renovate a worn object or place so it is clean, sound, and usable again.

See the word in context

The shop sells refurbished laptops — wiped, retested, fitted with new batteries — for half the price of new ones.

The roadside motel changed hands in March, and by June every room had been refurbished: carpets shampooed, tiles regrouted, mattresses swapped, each lamp rewired and each door eased back onto true hinges. Nothing about the place became fancy. It became clean, solid, and pleasant to walk into, which was the whole idea.

Refurbish is the exact word for that summer's work, because it means to restore something to good, clean condition — the object stays itself and gets its usefulness back. Spruce up stops at the surface, a scrub and a coat of paint; refurbishing goes as deep as the wiring.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    spruce up: Spruce up is surface-level freshening — a scrub, a coat of paint, done by lunch, with nothing behind the walls touched. Refurbish goes deeper: the whole worn object or room comes back to clean, sound, usable condition, down to the wiring if the wiring needs it.

    recondition: Recondition means to bring a machine back to sound working order — rebuilt motor, fresh seals, tested parts — and it is said mostly of machinery, with function its whole concern. Refurbish covers rooms and furniture as well as machines, and it cares about look and comfort along with working order.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    run down: let a place slide into shabbiness

    let rot: walk away and let it decay entirely

    Where it sits · how deep the renewal goes

    tidy up → refurbish → restore

    tidy up vs. refurbish: Tidy up is a far lighter pass than refurbish — sweep, dust, straighten, and the room merely looks cared for — while refurbishing makes the place sound and usable again, down to the hinges.

    restore vs. refurbish: Restore goes further than refurbish — every detail is returned to what the original makers intended, period materials and all, where refurbishing settles for clean, sound, and usable.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / practical

    Refurbish is for physical things brought back to clean working order: rooms, machines, furniture, fleets. Its restore-family unit-mates each hold a lane — rejuvenate makes a person or spirit feel young again, recapitulate re-walks the points of an argument, recalibrated trues an instrument to a standard. Refurbish takes the scrub brush: same object, good to look at and use again, with no claim about youth, accuracy, or argument.
