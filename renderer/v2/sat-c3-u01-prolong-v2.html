Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Prolong means to make something last longer.

See the word in context

The trainer iced the pitcher's shoulder between innings to prolong his night on the mound.

The conservators keep the watercolor room dim and cool, and rotate every sheet into dark storage after ninety days on the wall. Visitors grumble about the gloom. None of it makes the pigments one shade brighter; it prolongs their life, stretching perhaps two more centuries of visible color out of paper that sunlight would strip in a generation.

Prolong is the right verb because it means to make something last longer — it acts on time and states, never on physical distance. You can prolong an applause, a career, or a fading color, but not a hallway; a hallway gets lengthened. The dimness buys duration, and duration is all it buys.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    protract: To protract is to stretch something out until the stretching itself wearies everyone — protracted negotiations, a protracted lawsuit. Prolong is neutral about whether the extra time is welcome: you can prolong a life or a holiday gladly. Protract almost always complains.

    sustain: To sustain something is to keep it going at its current level — a singer sustains a note, a runner sustains a pace. It adds nothing beyond the effort of holding steady. Prolong is different: it makes a thing last longer than it otherwise would, pushing the end further away.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    shorten: to cut the length down

    cut short: to end a thing before its time

    Where it sits · how far past its natural length

    extend → prolong → drag out

    extend vs. prolong: Extend is the milder act — you can extend a deadline by one tidy day; prolong keeps the whole thing alive past its natural span.

    drag out vs. prolong: To drag out a meeting goes further than to prolong it: dragging out adds length nobody wants, long past any purpose the meeting had.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral

      Prolong works on time only: lives, waits, arguments, charges. Choose extend for boundaries and deadlines, lengthen for physical things, and protract when the stretching itself has become wearying. Its mirror in this unit is expedite — one stretches a thing's duration, the other clears its path — and note the tone swing: prolonging a life is a triumph, prolonging a meeting is a sin.
