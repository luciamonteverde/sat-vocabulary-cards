Meaning

What It Means

Recalibrated means adjusted again to restore accuracy.

See the word in context

After the furnace swap we recalibrated the old thermostat until its number finally agreed with the thermometer on the mantel.

Every spring the greenhouse crew recalibrated the carbon-dioxide sensor: a cylinder of gas mixed to a known concentration was opened beside it, and the sensor's dial was nudged until it reported exactly that figure. The tomatoes never knew the difference, but for another year the numbers in the log could be trusted.

Recalibrated is the exact word for that ritual, because it means adjusted again to restore accuracy — the settings are walked back to a known standard. Fine-tuned is gentler and needs no standard at all; you can fine-tune by feel, but you can only recalibrate against something known to be true.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    reset: Reset means sent back to a starting point — the trip counter zeroed, the router power-cycled. No standard is consulted and no accuracy is promised. Recalibrated makes the stronger claim: the settings were walked back against a known reference until the reading told the truth again.

    trued up: Trued up is the workshop phrase for bringing a thing back to exact — a wheel, a level, a saw fence, checked by hand and eye. Recalibrated is the instrument-room version of the same errand, and it always names a known standard the reading is matched against.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    misaligned: drifted away from the true reading

    neglected: left uncared for, never checked or adjusted

    Where it sits · how deep the fix reaches

    tweaked → recalibrated → overhauled

    tweaked vs. recalibrated: Tweaked is a far lighter fix than recalibrated — one knob nudged a hair by feel, nobody consulting anything — while recalibrating walks the settings back to a known standard.

    overhauled vs. recalibrated: Overhauled goes further than recalibrated — the machine comes apart and worn pieces are replaced, where recalibrating only walks the settings back to the standard.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral / technical

    Recalibrated belongs wherever an instrument meets a standard: scales, meters, sensors, gauges. For a casual tweak with no standard in sight, adjusted or fine-tuned is enough; recalibrated promises the reading was walked back to a reference and now tells the truth again. Its unit-mates reconfigures (new layout, same parts) and refurbish (clean and working again) fix different problems — accuracy is this word's whole job.
