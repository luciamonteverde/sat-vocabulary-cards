triumphant

/try-UM-fuhnt/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Triumphant means feeling or showing exultant joy over a victory or success — the win, worn openly.

See the word in context

Nadia's triumphant shout carried across the pool deck the instant the scoreboard confirmed her record.

When the appeals court read its decision, the young lawyer who had argued the case for free for six straight years walked out onto the courthouse steps, raised the ruling over her head, and let out a triumphant laugh that echoed off the columns. Strangers on the sidewalk found themselves cheering back before they even knew what she had won.

Two things make the moment triumphant rather than merely happy: there is a hard-fought victory behind it, and the joy is on full display — raised arms, a ringing laugh, a win worn in public.

    Accuracy

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    victorious: Victorious states a fact about the outcome: you won. Triumphant describes the joy that follows and shows — a team can be victorious on paper while only the player hoisting the trophy and beaming looks triumphant.

    jubilant: Jubilant means loudly, openly joyful about anything — a jubilant crowd may simply be glad it's Friday. Triumphant ties that joy to a win: it is jubilation with a victory behind it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    crestfallen: visibly deflated by defeat or disappointment

    defeated: beaten, and feeling the loss

    Where it sits · how openly the victory joy pours out

    pleased → triumphant → gloating

    pleased vs. triumphant: Pleased is weaker than triumphant: a pleased winner smiles quietly to herself, while a triumphant one lets the joy of victory pour out for everyone to see.

    gloating vs. triumphant: Gloating goes further than triumphant — and turns sour: triumphant joy simply overflows, but gloating aims that joy at the loser to rub the defeat in.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    loud, exultant — joy tied to a win

    Triumphant needs two ingredients: a victory behind the feeling and joy out where people can see it. That keeps it distinct from victorious, which only reports the result, and from this unit's ecstatic, which is overwhelming delight from any cause at all — a lottery winner is ecstatic, not triumphant, because nothing was contested. And when the display starts targeting the loser, the honest word is no longer triumphant but gloating.
