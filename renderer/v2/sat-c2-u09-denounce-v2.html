denounce

/dih-NOWNSS/ - rhymes with pounce

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Denounce means to criticize publicly.

See the word in context

It took the astronomy board less than a day to denounce the auction of forged meteorites being run under its name.

When the test-prep company was caught selling stolen exam questions, the tutors' guild did not send a quiet note. Its president stood before cameras to denounce the company by name, reading the findings aloud and declaring its conduct a betrayal of every student who ever studied honestly. By evening, three partner schools had cut ties.

Denounce is the precise word because the criticism is public, official, and aimed: a wrongdoer is named and held up for the world's judgment. To scold the company in a private letter would reach no one; to decry cheating in general would name no one. A denunciation points its finger in front of an audience.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    accuse: To accuse is to name a wrongdoer and a charge — and it can happen in a courtroom, a kitchen, or a private letter. Denounce needs more: a public stage, and a moral judgment pronounced from it, the wrong held up for an audience to condemn.

    attack: To attack is to assail in any register — fair or foul, whispered or broadcast — and the word says nothing about the setting or the speaker's standing. Denounce is narrower: condemnation delivered publicly and officially, meant to leave a mark before an audience.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    defend: speaks up for the accused

    commend: names someone for praise instead of blame

    Where it sits · how loudly and widely the protest sounds

    mutter → object → denounce

    mutter vs. denounce: To mutter against a decision is the faintest protest, complaint kept under the breath where it risks nothing; to denounce it is to condemn it at full volume, in front of everyone.

    object vs. denounce: To object is to state, in so many words, that you disagree; denounce goes further — it condemns in full voice, before an audience, and it means to leave a mark.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / public

    Denounce is condemnation with a podium under it: official, public, usually aimed at a named wrongdoer or a named wrong. Its unit-mate decry runs quieter and longer — a practice gets decried in columns and call-ins for years, while a scandal gets denounced by nightfall. If no audience is watching, you are merely criticizing.
