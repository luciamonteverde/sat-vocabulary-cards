remedial

/ri-MEE-dee-uhl/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Remedial means intended to correct or improve weak skills in a specific area, especially basic ones.

See the word in context

Twice a week, the third graders in the remedial reading program meet with a tutor who rebuilds their phonics skills step by step.

After three fender benders in the parking lot, the delivery company enrolled its newest drivers in a remedial driving course that drilled mirror checks and slow-speed turns.

The course exists for one reason: to fix the basic driving skills these drivers clearly never mastered. Instruction designed to correct a specific weakness in fundamental skills is exactly what remedial describes.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    corrective: Corrective means aimed at fixing any identified fault or error, and it is the standard word after inspections and audits — a corrective action plan. Remedial lives in the classroom: it describes instruction that repairs weak basic skills. Faults get corrective steps; skill gaps get remedial lessons.

    foundational: Foundational describes the most basic level of a subject — foundational math is where everyone starts. It says nothing about anything having gone wrong. Remedial is basic content with a mission: it exists because specific skills never took hold and must be rebuilt.

    catch-up: Catch-up work helps someone reach the level the rest of the group has already reached — extra sessions after a long absence, say. Remedial instruction is more diagnostic: it goes back and rebuilds specific weak skills, usually basic ones, rather than just covering missed ground.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    advanced: beyond the basic level

    accelerated: moving faster than the standard pace

    Where it sits · how demanding the coursework is

    refresher → remedial → intensive

    refresher vs. remedial: A refresher course is weaker than a remedial one; a refresher lightly reviews what students already know, while a remedial course rebuilds skills that never took hold.

    intensive vs. remedial: Intensive is stronger than remedial; a remedial class fixes weak basics at a steady pace, while an intensive program is a demanding, immersive overhaul of a subject.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    Neutral / academic

    Most common in education: remedial classes, remedial instruction, remedial lessons. Formal writing does sometimes use remedial action for steps taken to fix a problem, but after an inspection or audit the standard phrase is corrective action, not remedial. Do not confuse remedial with elementary, which describes content that is simply basic, not content specifically designed to repair a skill gap.
