predatory

/PRED-uh-tor-ee/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Predatory means living by hunting and killing other animals for food.

See the word in context

The dragonfly nymph is the pond's most predatory insect, taking tadpoles twice its own length.

A camera trap at a Kenyan waterhole records the same leopard on eleven nights. On six of them it stalks, kills, and eats an impala or a warthog; on the others it hunts without success and goes hungry. It never touches the carcasses the hyenas abandon nearby, and it eats no plant matter at all. The keeper's notes sum the animal up in a single word: predatory.

An animal that stalks and kills live prey on six nights out of eleven, refuses carrion, and eats no plants lives by hunting, which is what predatory means.

    Accuracy

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    hunting: Hunting names the activity of pursuing and killing prey, and any animal, or person, can spend one afternoon at it. Predatory describes what a creature is: an animal whose living depends on the hunt, a whole diet and way of life rather than an act.

    stalking: Stalking names one hunter's method -- following prey slowly and silently before the strike. Predatory covers the whole way of life of living by the kill, whether the killer stalks, ambushes, or runs its prey down in the open.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    herbivorous: feeding on plants

    scavenging: feeding on animals that are already dead

    Where it sits · how aggressively food is pursued

    carnivorous → predatory → rapacious

    carnivorous vs. predatory: Carnivorous says only that an animal eats meat, while predatory adds that it catches and kills that meat itself.

    rapacious vs. predatory: Rapacious is stronger and more judgemental than predatory, suggesting greed that runs well past need.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    scientific in biology; harshly critical in business and law

    In biology predatory means killing prey in order to eat it, while in ordinary use it accuses, as in predatory lending. Keep it apart from parasitic in this unit, since a predator kills its prey outright while a parasite feeds slowly on a host it keeps alive.
