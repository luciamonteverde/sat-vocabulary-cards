Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Attenuate means to reduce something in force or intensity; to weaken it.

See the word in context

A double row of poplars planted along the fence line attenuates the prairie wind before it reaches the orchard.

Divers carry lamps below thirty meters because seawater attenuates sunlight, filtering out the reds first and dimming the rest, until the brightest noon above becomes a blue-gray dusk.

Attenuate is the precise choice when something is deliberately or physically weakened as it passes through a barrier or medium. Unlike abate, which describes a force easing on its own, attenuate almost always has an agent — something does the weakening.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    weaken: Weaken is the plain, everyday word for making something less strong, and it claims nothing about how the loss happens. Attenuate is its technical relative: something attenuates a force by degrees, usually as the force passes through a barrier or medium, the way seawater attenuates sunlight.

    dilute: Dilute means to weaken something by mixing another substance into it, as water thins juice or a long meeting dilutes enthusiasm. To attenuate is to cut strength as something passes through a barrier; to dilute is to thin it by adding to the mixture itself.

    muffle: Muffle means to deaden sound specifically, usually by wrapping or covering the source — a scarf muffles a voice. Attenuate reaches far wider: signals, light, doses, and vibrations can all be attenuated, and the weakening is measured, not just hushed.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    boost: to raise strength or level, as an amplifier does a signal

    concentrate: to make stronger and denser rather than thinner

    Where it sits · how deeply the strength is cut

    dampen → attenuate → extinguish

    dampen vs. attenuate: Dampen is milder than attenuate — dampening a vibration takes the edge off it, while attenuating a signal can cut it to a small fraction of its strength.

    extinguish vs. attenuate: Extinguish is stronger than attenuate — an attenuated beam still gets through, weakened, but an extinguished one is gone entirely.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    technical / formal

    Attenuate belongs to physics, medicine, and engineering — signals, beams, doses, and vibrations are attenuated. In everyday speech, 'weaken' or 'dampen' is the natural choice; reach for attenuate when something passes through a barrier or medium and comes out the other side reduced. Compare abate, which is what a storm does on its own.

Researchers grew the pathogen for hundreds of generations in cells it was poorly suited to, and each cycle attenuated it a little further. By the fortieth generation the virus could still teach the immune system its shape, but its power to cause disease had fallen to a measured fraction of the original. What reached the clinic was the same virus in outline only — present, recognizable, and too feeble to do harm.
