Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Latent means present but hidden — really there, not yet showing itself, and capable of surfacing when conditions call it out.

See the word in context

The counter looked spotless until the technician dusted it for latent prints — marks laid down by a touch and unseen until the powder found them.

The infection can stay latent for a decade: carriers feel nothing, test clear on routine screens, and pass every physical — while the virus waits, whole and quiet, in the nerves.

Latent names exactly that interval. The thing is fully present — not approaching, not developing, already there — yet nothing on the surface betrays it. When symptoms finally arrive, nothing new has been added; what was hidden has simply stepped into view.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    dormant: Dormant means asleep — once active and able to wake again, like a volcano between eruptions or a club nobody has convened in years. Latent makes a different claim: the thing is genuinely there but has never yet shown itself at all.

    undeveloped: Undeveloped says only that the growth hasn't happened — undeveloped land, an undeveloped backhand. Latent goes further: the thing already exists, real and complete enough to surface, needing only the conditions that will call it out.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    manifest: fully shown — out where everyone can see it

    active: already at work, its effects in plain sight

    Where it sits · how close the hidden thing is to surfacing

    potential → latent → incipient

    potential vs. latent: Potential is weaker than latent: a potential talent might not exist at all — mere room for one — while a latent talent is really there, lying quiet and waiting.

    incipient vs. latent: Incipient is stronger than latent: what is latent still lies wholly out of sight, while an incipient change has broken the surface and faintly begun to show.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      clinical / analytical

      Latent is the laboratory's word for hiddenness — latent prints, latent infections, latent demand. It differs from its neighbors by what it claims: a potential talent might not exist yet; a dormant one has shown itself before and sleeps; a latent one is really there and has never yet surfaced. That 'really there' is the word's whole force — when the latent thing finally appears, nothing new has been created; something present all along has simply come into view.
