garrulous

/GAIR-uh-luhs/ - the stress falls on GAIR

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Garrulous means excessively talkative, especially about unimportant things.

See the word in context

The garrulous ranger turned a ten-minute trailhead briefing into an hour on squirrel politics and the full history of his hat.

Seat 14C taught Mira a lesson about red-eye flights: her garrulous seatmate narrated his fantasy-football roster, his sister's kitchen renovation, and the plot of a movie neither of them had seen, all before the drink cart reached row nine.

Garrulous is not a compliment: it means talkative past anyone's patience, usually about things that matter to no one else. The garrulous talker doesn't exchange conversation — he supplies all of it, in bulk, on topics of his own choosing.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    gabby: Gabby is the light, affectionate word for someone who talks a lot — the friendly neighbor who keeps you at the fence a few minutes too long. Garrulous is the heavyweight charge: talk that runs long past anyone's patience, mostly about trivia. Gabby teases; garrulous condemns.

    rambling: Rambling describes the path of the talk — wandering from topic to topic without direction — and even a short answer can ramble. Garrulous describes the person and the sheer quantity: too much talk, too little matter, going on long after every listener has given up.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    tight-lipped: deliberately saying as little as possible

    closemouthed: giving nothing away in conversation

    Where it sits · how far the talking outruns its purpose

    chatty → long-winded → garrulous

    chatty vs. garrulous: Chatty is far milder than garrulous — chatty company makes a pleasant hour, and nobody reaches for their headphones.

    long-winded vs. garrulous: Long-winded comes closer but still stops short of garrulous — a long-winded speaker takes too long to make a point, while a garrulous one keeps talking after the points have run out.

      critical / wry

      Garrulous criticizes: too much talk, too little matter. Loquacious (also in this unit) can stay neutral or even admiring about a fluent talker; garrulous never does — it adds tedium and triviality to the volume. The garrulous uncle isn't just talkative; he's still going, and it's about his lawn mower.
