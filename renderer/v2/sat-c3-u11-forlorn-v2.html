Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Forlorn means lonely, hopeless, or wearing the pitiful look of abandonment.

See the word in context

A single forlorn beach umbrella stood open in the November drizzle, weeks after the concession stand had closed.

By Friday the last stall in the emptied market looked forlorn: one crate of apples, a hand-lettered sign, and an owner who had stopped calling out to shoppers who never came.

Forlorn is more than 'alone.' It bundles three things — loneliness, abandonment, and a hopeless air — and it works as well for things as for people, which is why a single mitten on a fence can be forlorn.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    abandoned: Abandoned states the bare fact of being left behind — an owner gone, a claim given up — and says nothing about how the thing looks. Forlorn adds the pitiable air: lonely, hopeless, wearing its desertion openly. The fact is abandonment; the heartbreaking look of it is forlorn.

    dejected: Dejected means downcast after a particular disappointment — a lost match, a rejected application — and it belongs to people and their moods. Forlorn can settle on anything: a mitten, a platform, a person, each wearing the same lonely, hopeless look of being left behind. Dejection follows an event; forlornness is an air.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    hopeful: still expecting something better to come

    bustling: full of lively company and motion

    Where it sits · how total the abandonment feels

    lonely → forlorn → godforsaken

    lonely vs. forlorn: Lonely is milder than forlorn — a lonely evening passes, while forlorn adds abandonment and the sense that no one is coming.

    godforsaken vs. forlorn: Godforsaken goes further than forlorn — so utterly abandoned that even the hope of rescue feels absurd.

      Word Choice

      literary

      Forlorn paints lonely abandonment with a brush of hopelessness, and it works as well for objects and places as for people — a forlorn mitten, a forlorn platform at midnight. 'Lonely' names a feeling; forlorn paints the whole picture. Beside its unit-mates: bereft misses one precious thing, abject has lost even dignity — forlorn is the look of being left behind.
