What It Means

Misrepresent means to describe something in a false or misleading way.

See the word in context

The trailer misrepresented the film so thoroughly that audiences arrived expecting a comedy and sat through two hours of grief.

The pamphlet misrepresented the internship: 'newsroom experience' turned out to mean fetching coffee two floors below the newsroom, and the 'stipend' was a bus pass.

Misrepresent covers every flavor of false description — outright lies, half-truths, and truths arranged to deceive. Slanting merely tilts the emphasis, and falsifying changes the facts themselves; misrepresenting aims at the picture in the audience's head.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    mislead: To mislead is to aim at the audience's belief by any means — words, images, or well-chosen silence. Grammar tells the two verbs apart: you mislead a person, while you misrepresent the thing being described. Misleading needs no description at all; misrepresenting is deception built out of one.

    distort: To distort is to twist a true picture out of shape — stretching one part, shrinking another — the way a funhouse mirror handles a face. Misrepresent is the broader accusation: a description can misrepresent through outright lies, half-truths, or careful omission, with no twisting needed.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    set straight: correct a false impression once it exists

    report faithfully: describe a thing exactly as it is

    Where it sits · how deep the distortion goes

    slant → misrepresent → falsify

    slant vs. misrepresent: Slant is milder than misrepresent — a slanted account tilts the emphasis while keeping the picture recognizably true.

    falsify vs. misrepresent: Falsify goes further than misrepresent: to misrepresent is to arrange a false impression, while to falsify is to change the underlying facts themselves.

      Word Choice

      neutral / precise

      Misrepresent is the careful accusation: it says a description was false or misleading without claiming every word was a lie — which is why lawyers and fact-checkers lean on it. Fabricate, in this unit, builds falsehood outright; misrepresent can work entirely with truths, arranged to deceive.
