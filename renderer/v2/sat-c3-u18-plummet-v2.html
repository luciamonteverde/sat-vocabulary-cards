plummet

/PLUM-it/ - rhymes with summit

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Plummet means to fall steeply and very fast.

See the word in context

When the crane's cable slipped, the piano plummeted six stories and burst on the pavement like a dropped crate of bells.

Reservoir engineers watched the gauge as the dam's outflow gates opened: the lake level did not ease down, it plummeted, dropping a boat-ramp's length in a single afternoon.

Plummet is the word for falls with no brakes — steep, fast, and usually alarming. A reading, a reputation, or a climber can plummet, but only when the drop is sheer; for a slow slide downward, this unit's wane tells the truer story.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    plunge: A plunge is just as steep and fast as a plummet, but it can be chosen: a hand plunged into icy water, a country plunged into reform by its own vote. Plummet keeps no such option — what plummets is falling helplessly, like a stone let go.

    nosedive: A nosedive borrows the aircraft's picture — a nose tipping down into a sudden steep drop — and it is the favorite word for numbers: sales, ratings, stocks. Plummet describes the same steepness for anything at all, and its falls read as sheer and unbroken rather than tipped.

    crash: A crash is a fall named by its ending — the impact, the wreck, the ruin at the bottom. Plummet is the fall itself, the sheer unbraked descent, and says nothing about how it ends; a market can plummet for a week before anyone calls it a crash.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    soar: to climb high and fast, or glide effortlessly upward

    inch up: to rise by tiny, patient amounts

    Where it sits · how severe the drop is

    slip → tumble → plummet

    slip vs. plummet: A slip is far milder than a plummet — a small loss of footing or a few points shed, quickly caught.

    tumble vs. plummet: A tumble is milder than a plummet — a real fall, but a bumping, partial one, while a plummet drops sheer and unchecked to the bottom.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      vivid / journalistic

      Plummet is a headline verb — it makes a fall dramatic. Save it for genuine dives: a stock, a temperature, a falling climber. If the movement is slow erosion, wane or dwindle usually tells it straighter; plummet claims a drop that is steep, fast, or dizzying in its sheer extent.
