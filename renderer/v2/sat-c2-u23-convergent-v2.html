convergent

/kun-VUR-junt/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Convergent means coming together toward the same point or answer from different starting places.

See the word in context

The rival startups drew up convergent designs for the delivery drone, each arriving at the same four-rotor frame before either had seen the other's plans.

Dolphins are mammals and ichthyosaurs were reptiles, yet the fossil casts show the same torpedo body and the same tail-driven stroke. Biologists call it convergent evolution: two distant lineages arriving at one design, because the sea poses every fast swimmer the same problem.

Convergent describes lines, paths, or conclusions that start far apart and close toward the same point. The separate starting places are half the meaning — things that were never apart have nothing to close — and the drawing-together is the other half.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    intersecting: Intersecting paths simply cross once, at whatever angle, and carry on their separate ways. Convergent paths are aimed at the same point — the whole story is the closing distance.

    aligned: Aligned means already in agreement, sitting on the same line. Convergent stresses the approach: different starting places, with the gap still closing.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    divergent: bending apart from a common point

    scattered: spread in all directions, with no common point at all

    Where it sits · how far separate paths have closed toward one point

    similar → convergent → merged

    similar vs. convergent: Similar is weaker than convergent: similar findings merely resemble one another, while convergent findings are actively closing on the same answer from different directions.

    merged vs. convergent: Merged goes further than convergent — convergent paths are still distinct as they close in, but merged ones have already collapsed into a single line.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    technical / analytic

    Convergent is the analyst's adjective — evidence, evolution, opinions, lines. Use it when separate starting points are closing on one answer, and let the unit-mates handle the aftermath: a confluence is the meeting itself, and things that coalesce have already fused. Convergent is the approach, still in motion.
