Meaning

What It Means

Adaptable means capable of adjusting to new conditions.

See the word in context

The pothos is a famously adaptable houseplant, growing just as happily in a dim hallway as on a bright windowsill.

Marisol, the softball team's utility player, is prized for being adaptable: in a single weekend tournament she played shortstop, catcher, and left field.

Each position demands different skills and instincts, and Marisol adjusts to whichever one the team needs from game to game. Adjusting successfully to changing demands is exactly what adaptable means.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    versatile: Versatile means useful or skilled in many different roles — a versatile player covers several positions. Adaptable is about adjusting rather than multiplying uses: an adaptable tool may do one job while retuning itself to conditions. Versatile counts the roles; adaptable describes the adjusting.

    malleable: Malleable means easily shaped or reshaped by an outside force, the way warm metal takes whatever form the hammer gives it. An adaptable person or design does the adjusting itself, actively reshaping how it works to fit new conditions. Malleable things are changed by others; adaptable things change themselves.

    adjustable: Adjustable means built with settings someone can change by hand — an adjustable wrench, an adjustable desk. The change comes from whoever turns the dial. Adaptable goes further: an adaptable system or person responds to new conditions on its own, without waiting to be reset.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    rigid: refusing to bend or change

    inflexible: unable to adjust to new conditions

    Where it sits · how thoroughly you change to fit circumstances

    flexible → adaptable → protean

    flexible vs. adaptable: Flexible is weaker than adaptable, a flexible person will bend when plans change, while an adaptable person actively reshapes how they work to fit the new situation.

    protean vs. adaptable: Protean is stronger than adaptable, an adaptable colleague adjusts to change, while a protean one seems to transform completely for every new role.

    Word Choice

    Neutral / everyday

    Common in everyday and workplace English, often in the pattern adaptable to, as in adaptable to change. Watch the spelling trap with adoptable, which differs by one letter but means suitable to be adopted, as with a shelter pet.
