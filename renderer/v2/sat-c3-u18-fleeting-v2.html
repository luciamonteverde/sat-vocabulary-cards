Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Fleeting means lasting only an instant and gone before you can hold it — over almost as soon as it is noticed.

See the word in context

From the train window he caught a fleeting sight of the sea — a wedge of silver between two hills — before the tunnel shut it away.

Fame from the viral clip proved fleeting: the interview requests stopped inside a month, and by the new year strangers had gone back to walking past her at the grocery store.

Fleeting does more than measure a short time — it watches the thing go. Built on an old verb for floating and flowing, it pictures the moment streaming past like water: in sight, then carried off. That is why it attaches to glimpses, expressions, chances, and fame, and not to short things that sit obediently on a schedule.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    momentary: lasting just a moment, as a fact of the clock — a momentary delay, a momentary lapse. Fleeting adds escape to the shortness: the moment streams past before you can hold it.

    ephemeral: short-lived by nature, born to die soon — mayflies, fashions, morning frost. Fleeting is quicker still and more personal: it is how the instant feels as it slips through your fingers.

    transient: staying only a short time before moving on — transient guests, the transient trade of a port town. Fleeting is not about residence at all: it times the moment itself, not the stay.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    enduring: lasting on and on, holding up under the years

    permanent: fixed in place for good

    Where it sits · how fast the moment slips away

    passing → fleeting → evanescent

    passing vs. fleeting: Passing is weaker than fleeting — a passing mood can take a whole afternoon to move through, while a fleeting one is gone in the space of a glance.

    evanescent vs. fleeting: Evanescent is stronger than fleeting — a fleeting moment at least arrives whole before it goes, while an evanescent one is already dissolving as it appears, like breath on cold glass.

    Section 3

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral, faintly wistful — narration, essays, reviews

    Fleeting is for what escapes: glimpses, expressions, chances, fame. If the shortness sits on a schedule — a brief meeting, a short delay — brief is the plainer, better word; fleeting implies you reached for the moment and it had already gone. And for people or crowds that come and go rather than moments, transient does that work. Fleeting looks a little backward as it points, always with a note of loss.
