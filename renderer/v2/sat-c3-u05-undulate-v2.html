undulate

/UN-dyuh-layt/ - the stress falls on UN

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Undulate means to move in a smooth, wavelike motion.

See the word in context

All night the green curtains of the aurora undulated above the fjord.

Wind moved through the barley in long pulses until the whole hillside undulated — one slow wave chasing another toward the fence line.

Undulate is the smooth, continuous, full-length wave — a flag, a jellyfish, a wheat field — never a jerk or a wobble. And note the disguise: the un- is not a prefix. The word is Latin unda, 'wave,' plus endings; nothing is being negated, and there is no 'dulate' underneath.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    ripple: A ripple is a small surface wave -- undulate on a miniature scale, the teacup version. Both motions travel, but ripple stays tiny and local: a pebble's rings, a shiver across a pond. When the wave runs full-length through a field or a flag, the word is undulate.

    sway: To sway is one body rocking side to side -- a mast, a dancer, a drunk -- with no wave travelling through it. Undulate needs the wave itself: motion that moves along a length, section after section. A lamppost sways in the wind; the wheat field undulates.

    roll: To roll is the broad heave of a whole mass moving at once -- the deck tilts, the fog bank advances as one body. Undulate is motion in successive waves, each section rising as the last falls. The sea rolls under a ship; an eel undulates through it.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    stiffen: to go rigid — all wave motion lost

    freeze: to stop moving altogether

    Where it sits · how large and wild the motion grows

    tremble → undulate → churn

    tremble vs. undulate: To tremble is a faint, shivering motion — it stops short of undulate, which rolls in full, smooth waves.

    churn vs. undulate: Churn goes further than undulate: undulating water rolls in even waves, while churning water boils over itself in violent confusion.

      Section 3

      Apply

      “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

      Word Choice

      neutral

      Undulate is halfway literary — reach for it when motion travels in smooth waves along a whole length: fields, flags, eels, hills (even a road's dips can undulate). Ripple is smaller, sway is side-to-side, wobble is unsteady. For choppy violence, churn.
