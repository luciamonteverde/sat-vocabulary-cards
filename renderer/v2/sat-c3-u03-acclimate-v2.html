Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Acclimate means to become accustomed to a new climate or set of conditions.

See the word in context

It took the night crew a week to acclimate to the graveyard shift, but soon the 2 a.m. start felt as ordinary as breakfast.

Seedlings raised under glass will scorch in an open field, so the gardener sets the trays outside for an hour longer each day, letting the plants acclimate to wind and direct sun before transplanting.

Acclimate is more precise than adjust because it names a gradual, whole-system settling into new conditions — the plant, or the person, keeps changing until a once-harsh environment feels normal, not merely endured.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    adapt: To adapt is to change your methods, gear, or habits so they fit a new demand; it covers any change made to fit new needs. Acclimate is narrower: you redesign nothing and instead grow used to conditions little by little until they feel normal.

    settle in: Settling in is the friendly, everyday phrase for getting comfortable somewhere new: unpacking, learning names, finding your favorite seat. Acclimate is more bodily and gradual — your whole system keeps shifting until a once-harsh climate or schedule finally feels normal, whether or not you feel at home yet.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    resist: to fight the new conditions instead of settling into them

    rebel: to push back openly against what you are asked to live with

    Where it sits · how natural the new surroundings feel

    cope → adjust → acclimate

    cope vs. acclimate: Coping is far weaker than acclimating — you grit your teeth and get through days that still feel foreign, while a person who has acclimated barely notices the same conditions.

    adjust vs. acclimate: Adjusting means making small deliberate changes — a new alarm, a warmer coat — but it stops short of acclimating, where the new conditions finally feel like home.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    neutral

    Acclimate is the word for settling into new conditions gradually — an altitude, a schedule, a workplace. For a one-time tweak, adjust is plainer. Note how it divides labor with its unit-mate withstand: acclimating changes you until the conditions feel normal; withstanding holds you unchanged against them.
