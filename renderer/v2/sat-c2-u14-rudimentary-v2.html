Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Rudimentary means basic and undeveloped.

See the word in context

Their heist plan was rudimentary — wear overalls, look bored, walk the painting out the front.

The flightless beetle still carries wings. Under the shell lie two stubs a millimeter long, veined like the real thing, folded as if for a flight they will never take. Naturalists call them rudimentary wings: the first shape of the thing, never finished — a sketch the species stopped drawing a hundred thousand generations ago.

Rudimentary is the exact word because the wings are basic and undeveloped — the beginnings of a thing, not the thing grown. That's the word's center of gravity: a rudimentary skill, kit, or system has the working shape and none of the development. It is not an insult; every finished thing was once a beginning. It is simply a reading of how far from finished the thing stands.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    primitive: Primitive points backward: an early, crude stage of something that has since advanced — a primitive engine, primitive surgery. Rudimentary needs no history; it says this thing, here and now, is basic and undeveloped for its purpose. A brand-new app can be rudimentary without ever being primitive.

    crude: Crude judges the workmanship: rough, unrefined, unfinished at the edges, whatever the stage of development. Rudimentary judges the development itself — the working shape exists and the growth has not happened yet. A crude thing was made roughly; a rudimentary one simply has not grown up.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    advanced: far along in development

    sophisticated: developed until even the fine points are handled

    Where it sits · how much substance is actually there

    simple → rudimentary → skeletal

    simple vs. rudimentary: A simple tool is plain but complete — nothing missing, nothing extra; rudimentary means the development itself hasn't happened yet.

    skeletal vs. rudimentary: Skeletal goes past rudimentary — not underdeveloped but stripped to the bare frame, ribs with nothing on them.

    Word Choice

    neutral / matter-of-fact

    Rudimentary measures development, not worth: a rudimentary skill or setup has the shape and awaits the growth. Two neighbors to keep straight: elementary describes the ground floor itself — first-grade arithmetic is elementary and exactly as it should be — while rudimentary implies the thing is undergrown for its purpose. And this unit's inept is harsher still: rudimentary skill is on its way; inept performance has arrived and failed.
