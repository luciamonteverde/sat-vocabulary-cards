prerequisite

/pree-REK-wuh-zit/

Section 1

Meaning

“What does this word mean?”

Hear It

What It Means

Prerequisite means something that is required as a prior condition before something else can happen or exist.

See the word in context

Passing the written traffic exam is a prerequisite for earning a learner's permit.

Passing the pool's swim test is a prerequisite for joining the kayaking class, so no one touches a paddle until they can tread water for two minutes.

The swim test must be completed before the class is even allowed to begin. A condition that comes first and unlocks what follows is a prerequisite.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    requirement: A requirement is anything that must be met or done, whenever it applies — before, during, or after. A prerequisite is a specific kind of requirement: one that must be satisfied before something else can begin. Every prerequisite is a requirement, but a rule that applies the whole time through is a requirement, not a prerequisite.

    precondition: A precondition is a state of affairs that must exist beforehand — trust is a precondition for peace talks. It is abstract and can simply describe how things stand. A prerequisite is usually a concrete, checkable hurdle someone must clear — a course passed, a test taken — before the next step opens.

    qualification: A qualification is a skill or credential a person carries — a license, a degree, five years' experience. It belongs to someone. A prerequisite belongs to a process: the prior condition that must be met before the next thing can start. Your qualifications may satisfy a program's prerequisites, but the words point at different things.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    option: something you are free to choose or skip

    luxury: something pleasant but not needed

    Where it sits · how strictly it must be met

    suggestion → prerequisite → necessity

    suggestion vs. prerequisite: Suggestion is weaker: a suggestion can be ignored, but a prerequisite must be met before you can move forward at all.

    necessity vs. prerequisite: Necessity is stronger and broader: a necessity is required no matter what, while a prerequisite is required specifically before a particular next step.
