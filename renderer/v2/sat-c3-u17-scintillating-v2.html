What It Means

Scintillating means sparkling with quick, dancing points of light — and, of lively talk or wit, flashing with the same brilliance.

See the word in context

The chandelier's ten thousand crystals kept the ballroom scintillating through the last waltz.

Guests fought for chairs near the playwright, whose scintillating table talk threw off puns, riddles, and comebacks like sparks from a grinding wheel.

Scintillating is precise in both senses at once: literally, light broken into quick dancing points; figuratively, wit that flashes the same way. Steady brilliance would be radiance — scintillating is brilliance that keeps sparking.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    sparkling: Sparkling is the everyday twin — quick points of light dancing off water, glass, or lively talk. Scintillating means the same motion with more polish and more voltage: the sparks come thick and fast, and the word itself dresses up, especially for brilliant conversation. Cider sparkles; a great wit scintillates.

    glittering: Glittering is hard, bright flashing — sequins, frost, a case of diamonds — and it can feel cold or even suspect, as in glittering prizes. Scintillating keeps the flash but warms it with life and wit. Glittering light impresses the eye from a distance; scintillating light, or talk, delights the company.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    matte: a flat finish that returns no light

    leaden: heavy, gray, and lightless

    Where it sits · how much the light dances

    gleaming → glinting → scintillating

    gleaming vs. scintillating: Gleaming is one steady, soft shine — a well-kept floor; it stops far short of scintillating.

    glinting vs. scintillating: Glinting goes further than gleaming — a hard flash now and then — but stops short of scintillating, an unbroken shower of sparks.

    Word Choice

    admiring

    Scintillating praises anything that throws off sparks — a frost-lit field or a dinner guest's wit. Its partner and foil in this unit is effulgent: effulgent light pours out in one steady flood, scintillating light dances in points. Sparkling says the same thing more casually; scintillating adds polish, especially for brilliant talk.
