/lej-er-duh-MAYN/ - the stress falls on MAYN

Meaning

Hear It

What It Means

Legerdemain means skillful trickery that hides what is really happening.

See the word in context

Getting the bill through both chambers took parliamentary legerdemain: the clause everyone opposed passed at midnight as a 'technical correction.'

Museum-goers never noticed the swap: with a little curatorial legerdemain, the fragile original went to the vault and a labeled cast took its place mid-afternoon, in full view of a busy gallery.

Legerdemain is trickery raised to a craft — so smooth that the moment of the trick cannot be seen. Ordinary trickery can be clumsy and still work, and a dodge merely evades; legerdemain, French for 'light of hand,' is reserved for moves executed with a conjurer's polish.

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    sleight of hand: Sleight of hand is the literal English for legerdemain — the conjurer's physical move, quick fingers passing a coin while the audience watches the other hand. Legerdemain has traveled further from the stage: accountants, lawyers, and politicians can practice legerdemain entirely on paper, where no hand ever moves.

    misdirection: Misdirection is the technique of steering your eyes to the wrong place at the right moment, so the move happens where you are not looking. Legerdemain names the whole polished performance; misdirection is one tool inside it, and it only works while the audience is looking away.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    full disclosure: lays every step out to be inspected

    clumsiness: hands that hide nothing because they cannot

    Where it sits · how polished the deception is

    dodge → trickery → legerdemain

    dodge vs. legerdemain: A dodge is far milder than legerdemain — a schoolboy's dodge is a single evasive hop, artless and usually obvious.

    trickery vs. legerdemain: Trickery stops short of legerdemain: trickery can be clumsy and still succeed, while legerdemain is trickery polished until the trick itself cannot be seen.

    Apply

    “Can I recognize and use it correctly?”

    Word Choice

    formal / showy

    Legerdemain is a flourish of a word — use it when trickery is executed with a performer's skill, in accounting, law, or politics as easily as on a stage. Trickery is the plain word; ruse, in this same unit, names one specific trick rather than the artistry. Nobody says legerdemain about a clumsy lie.
