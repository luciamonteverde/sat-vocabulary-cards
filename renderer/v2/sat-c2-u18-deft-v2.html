What It Means

Deft means quick and skillful in one's movements, especially with the hands.

See the word in context

The florist's deft wiring saved the drooping peonies an hour before the gallery opening.

At the oyster bar, Kofi shucks to order: a deft slide of the knife at the hinge, a quarter-turn, and the shell opens unbroken with the oyster untouched in its liquor — two hundred a night, and the count of cut thumbs, he says, stands at zero since the nineties.

Deft is the exact word because deft means skillful and capable, especially with the hands — small, precise motions that land right the first time. Quick alone could be sloppy; strong is beside the point. Deft is economy: no wasted move, no bruised oyster, no blood.

    Compare the words

    Synonyms · words with a similar meaning

    sure-handed: Sure-handed praises reliability under pressure — the hands do not slip when the stakes rise. Deft asks for more than a good grip: quick, economical, precise motion. A sure-handed mover gets the mirror down the stairs intact; deft fingers retie the bow before anyone sees it slipped.

    nimble-fingered: Nimble-fingered praises pure finger speed — fast, light movement and nothing else. Deft keeps the quickness but adds tidy economy: each motion is placed and none is wasted. A nimble-fingered shuffler is merely fast; a deft one makes every flick land exactly where it was aimed.

    What it isn’t · antonyms and near-opposites

    fumbling: hands that drop what they hold

    butterfingered: the playground word for the same failing

    Where it sits · how much precision the fingers show

    handy → deft → surgical

    handy vs. deft: Handy fixes the hinge and hangs the shelf — useful skill with no special finesse claimed — but deft goes further: quick, precise motions with nothing wasted.

    surgical vs. deft: Surgical goes further than deft — deft hands work clean and quick; surgical precision could part a knot one thread at a time.

    Word Choice

    neutral / physical

    Deft lives closest to the fingers — knots, sutures, pastry, solder. Of its unit-mates, adroit adds cleverness and stretches to social moves, agile is the whole body at speed, adept is mastery of an entire pursuit. If you could film the praise in close-up on two hands, the word is deft.
